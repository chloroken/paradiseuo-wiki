# Resisting Spells
Resisting Spells gives a chance to shrug off part of a hostile spell. A resisted spell does three quarters of its damage, or lasts three quarters of its duration.

## Resistible Spells
- Magic Arrow
- Harm
- Fireball
- Poison
- Lightning
- Mana Drain
- Mind Blast
- Paralyze
- Energy Bolt
- Explosion
- Chain Lightning
- Flame Strike
- Mana Vampire
- Meteor Swarm
