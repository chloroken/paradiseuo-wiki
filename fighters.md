# Fighters
Fighters always take a weapon skill and [Tactics](/tactics), and most take [Parrying](/parrying) and [Anatomy](/anatomy) for the rest of [Valor](/valor).

| Class | Unique Qualities |
|:-|:-:|
| Armsmaster | Stacks weapon effects that weaken, slow, and drain one target |
| Prospector | Fights with a pickaxe that hits twice as hard, and mines its own ore |
| Warrior | Gets full Valor with a sword and shield and a bow, without Arms Lore |
| Woodsman | Does the highest damage of any class, and chops wood |

## Armsmaster
The Armsmaster is a weapons expert who carries a small armory and wears a monster down instead of out-damaging it. [Arms Lore](/arms_lore) powers most [weapon effects](/weapon_effects), and [Swordsmanship](/swordsmanship) and [Mace Fighting](/mace_fighting) cover nearly every weapon that has one, so it can stack several on the same target.

A one-handed mace [saps its vigor](/weapon_effects#sapping-vigor), so its melee hits land as if it were weaker. A war hammer's [Crushing Blow](/weapon_effects#crushing-blow) halves its attack speed, and a polearm's [Concussion Blow](/weapon_effects#concussion-blow) halves a caster's Intelligence, using Anatomy instead of Arms Lore. Each effect lasts a while, so it can land one, switch weapons, and stack the next. A drained, slowed monster hits far softer and far less often. Swords add [critical strikes](/weapon_effects#critical-strikes), but those only match the steady damage of weapons with no effect, so they're a side benefit. Its Anatomy gives it Valor's regeneration and stronger [bandages](/healing) that cure poison.

It skips Parrying to fit both weapon skills, so it gets none of Valor's toughness and has no use for a shield. It takes the full weight of every hit until its effects land, and juggling weapons takes attention and backpack space.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `100` Dexterity, `25` Intelligence
- Skills: Swordsmanship, Mace Fighting, Arms Lore, Tactics, Anatomy, Healing, and Resisting Spells
- Gear: swords, polearms, maces, and war hammers, with plate
- Defense: its weapon skills, Valor's accuracy and regeneration, and resisted spells
- Healing: bandages
- Weakness: no Parrying, so it gets none of Valor's toughness

## Warrior
The Warrior is the most well-rounded fighter, and the easiest to play well. It has full Valor and needs no Arms Lore, because it uses weapons without weapon effects. A kryss or war fork with a shield are the strongest one-handed weapons that have no effect to pay for.

Up close, it fights sword and board: fast fencing hits, shield blocks, and every Valor bonus. When something stays out of reach, it switches to a bow, since [Archery](/archery) lets it fire from range with the same Tactics and Anatomy bonuses. Its bandages heal well and cure poison. It can handle almost anything reasonably well, without any one weakness for monsters to exploit.

What it trades for that reliability is anything special. It has no weapon effects, no magic, no crafting, and no way to control a fight, so it relies on doing steady damage and taking it well.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `100` Dexterity, `25` Intelligence
- Skills: Fencing, Archery, Tactics, Anatomy, Parrying, Healing, and Resisting Spells
- Gear: kryss or war fork, shield, and bow, with plate
- Defense: Fencing, shield blocks, full Valor, and resisted spells
- Healing: bandages
- Weakness: no weapon effects and no utility

## Woodsman
The Woodsman is the hardest hitter in the game. [Lumberjacking](/lumberjacking) adds up to `30%` damage to axes, on top of Tactics, Strength, and Anatomy, and a two-handed axe's big hits make the most of it. It chops wood with the same axe, so it gathers lumber as easily as it fights.

It plays like a berserker: close the distance and swing as hard as possible. Axes have no weapon effect, so it needs no Arms Lore and spends the points on full Valor instead. Its hits with an axe also train Lumberjacking, so it grows into its damage just by fighting.

A two-handed axe leaves no hand for a shield, so it takes Parrying only for Valor's toughness and never blocks. It has no utility beyond damage, and plenty of hits get through, so it leans on bandages and regeneration between fights.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `100` Dexterity, `25` Intelligence
- Skills: Lumberjacking, Swordsmanship, Tactics, Anatomy, Parrying, Healing, and Resisting Spells
- Gear: two-handed axes, with plate
- Defense: Swordsmanship, full Valor, and resisted spells
- Healing: bandages
- Weakness: no hands for a shield and no utility, so it only takes Parrying for Valor's toughness
