# Quests
*Paradise* has the following quests:

- **Escort NPCs**
	- double click the `NPC`
- **Seekers of Adventure**
	- double click the `NPC`
- **Prison Camps** (escort or seeker)
	- say `destination`
  - say `i will take thee`
- **Witch's Apprentice**
	- double click `Grizelda`
