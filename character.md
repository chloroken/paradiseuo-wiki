# Character
Every character is built from three stats and up to `700` skill points. These pages cover how to build one, some class ideas to start with, and the reputation a character earns along the way. See [Reputation](/reputation).

## Building a Character
- [Stats](/stats): Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence, and what each one does.
- [Skills](/skills): every skill, including the ones that are disabled.

## Classes
Ultima Online doesn't have discrete classes. Instead, players choose stats and skills to create a build. For example, to play a fighter, one would spend stat points on Strength and Dexterity, and skill points on weapon, defensive, and healing skills.

During character creation there is a profession screen, but it should be ignored and `Advanced` should always be selected because *Paradise* targets an era that predates that screen. The following classes are just ideas, many of them rooted in classic T2A gameplay.

| Class | Weapons | Skills | What Makes It Unique |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Artisan | Spellbook | Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Carpentry, Inscription, Magery, Tailoring, Tinkering | Makes gear, potions, and scrolls for everyone else, and Recalls between workshops. |
| Assassin | Fencing weapons | Fencing, Tactics, Anatomy, Poisoning, Hiding, Stealth, Resisting Spells | The melee ambusher: a poisoned opener from stealth for up to 2.5× damage, then steady poison that keeps casters busy curing. |
| Battlemage | Halberd | Swordsmanship, Tactics, Anatomy, Magery, Evaluating Intelligence, Meditation, Resisting Spells | The biggest burst in the game, lining up spells with a halberd swing, while Concussion Blow drains enemy casters' mana. |
| Beastmaster | Instrument | Animal Taming, Animal Lore, Veterinary, Musicianship, Peacemaking, Provocation, Hiding | One of the strongest hunters, with pets and provoked monsters fighting for it, but no Magery or Valor to fall back on. |
| Deadeye | Bow | Archery, Tactics, Anatomy, Tracking, Fletching, Healing, Resisting Spells | The longest range and fastest shots after moving, and shots against fleeing targets never miss. |
| Druid | Spellbook | Animal Taming, Animal Lore, Veterinary, Magery, Evaluating Intelligence, Meditation, Resisting Spells | A pet army backed by offensive spells and Recall, healing pets from range with bandages. |
| Forager | Axe, pickaxe | Mining, Lumberjacking, Fishing, Swordsmanship, Tactics, Magery 50, Hiding 50 | Gathers every raw material, defends itself with the boosted axe or pickaxe it already carries, and Recalls home with the load. |
| Minstrel | Bow, instrument | Musicianship, Peacemaking, Provocation, Discordance, Archery, Tactics, Healing | Controls the battlefield with all three bard skills, then finishes enemies with a bow and Valor accuracy. |
| Monk | Fists | Magery, Evaluating Intelligence, Meditation, Wrestling, Anatomy, Tactics, Resisting Spells | Fights unarmed in leather with no mana penalty, and its punches stun any target, armed or not. |
| Paladin | One-handed mace and shield | Mace Fighting, Anatomy, Healing, Parrying, Tactics, Resisting Spells, Magery 40, Meditation 60 | A mace tank with healing and buff spells that still keeps most of Valor, sapping monsters' vigor. |
| Pirate | Instrument, spellbook | Magery, Meditation, Musicianship, Provocation, Cartography, Lockpicking, Hiding | Runs a whole treasure hunt alone, from farming maps to provoking the guardians and opening the chest. |
| Prospector | Pickaxe and shield | Mining, Swordsmanship, Tactics, Anatomy, Parrying, Healing, Resisting Spells | The only fighter that pairs top-tier damage with a heater shield, thanks to the one-handed pickaxe and Mining's 100% bonus. |
| Rogue | Knife, sword | Swordsmanship, Tactics, Anatomy, Hiding, Stealth, Poisoning, Lockpicking | Opens with a poisoned knife ambush, switches to a sword for crits, and picks dungeon chests along the way. |
| Shadow | Heavy crossbow, bow | Archery, Tactics, Anatomy, Hiding, Stealth, Healing, Resisting Spells | The ranged ambusher: a heavy crossbow shot from stealth for 2.5× that can't miss, then a bow for steady damage. |
| Shepherd | Shepherd's crook | Herding, Mace Fighting, Tactics, Anatomy, Parrying, Healing, Resisting Spells | Hit-and-run with the shillelagh: a 10× bonk every 9 seconds that never misses at grandmaster. |
| Smith | One-handed mace and shield | Blacksmithing, Mining, Mace Fighting, Tactics, Anatomy, Parrying, Healing | A self-sufficient tank with a boosted mace that saps vigor, and it mines, makes, and repairs its own gear. |
| Summoner | Spellbook | Magery, Meditation, Evaluating Intelligence, Anatomy, Inscription, Resisting Spells, Spirit Speak | Fields all four elementals at once, with summons that last twice as long, behind strong Reactive Armor and Protection. |
| Warden | Quarter staff, club and wooden shield | Carpentry, Mace Fighting, Tactics, Anatomy, Parrying, Healing, Resisting Spells | Switches between a boosted staff with Crushing Blow and a turtle stance behind a heater-strength wooden shield. |
| Warrior | Sword and shield, bow | Swordsmanship, Archery, Tactics, Anatomy, Parrying, Healing, Resisting Spells | The toughest fighter, with full Valor and no Magery, and a bow for anything out of reach. |
| Woodsman | Two-handed axe | Lumberjacking, Swordsmanship, Tactics, Anatomy, Parrying, Healing, Resisting Spells | The highest damage in the game, from two-handed axes with the full Lumberjacking bonus and Concussion Blow. |
