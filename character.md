# Character
Every character is built from three stats and up to `700` skill points. These pages cover how to build one, some class ideas to start with, and the reputation a character earns along the way. See [Reputation](/reputation).

## Building a Character
- [Stats](/stats): Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence, and what each one does.
- [Skills](/skills): every skill, including the ones that are disabled.

## Classes
Ultima Online doesn't have discrete classes. Instead, players choose stats and skills to create a build. For example, to play a fighter, one would spend stat points on Strength and Dexterity, and skill points on weapon, defensive, and healing skills.

The following classes are just ideas, many of them rooted in classic T2A gameplay.

### Mages
Mages use [Anatomy](/anatomy) with [Evaluating Intelligence](/evaluating_intelligence) instead of [Wrestling](/wrestling) to defend. See [Wrestling: Unarmed Defense](/wrestling#unarmed-defense).

| Class | Strength<br>Dexterity<br>Intelligence | Skills | Weapons | Description |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Battlemage | 100<br>25<br>100 | Swordsmanship<br>Tactics<br>Arms Lore<br>Magery<br>Evaluating Intelligence<br>Meditation<br>Resisting Spells | Polearms | The biggest burst in the game, lining up spells like Explosion and Energy Bolt with a polearm swing, while Concussion Blow drains enemy casters' mana. While holding the polearm, defends with Swordsmanship. |
| Monk | 100<br>25<br>100 | Magery<br>Evaluating Intelligence<br>Meditation<br>Wrestling<br>Anatomy<br>Tactics<br>Resisting Spells | Fists | Fights with fists and magic, wearing leather armor so its mana regeneration isn't slowed. Its punches stun targets in place to control the fight. |
| Summoner | 100<br>25<br>100 | Magery<br>Meditation<br>Evaluating Intelligence<br>Anatomy<br>Inscription<br>Resisting Spells<br>Spirit Speak | Spellbooks | Spirit Speak strengthens its summons and lets it field all four elementals at once, while Inscription and Meditation make its Reactive Armor and Protection unusually strong. |
