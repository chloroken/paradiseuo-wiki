# Magic Reflection
Magic Reflection gives the caster a buff that reflects the next offensive spell. It lasts until a spell is reflected, and can't be recast while it's already in effect.

| Property | Value |
|:-|:-:|
| Circle | 5 |
| Mana | 14 |
| Duration | Until used |

## Spells Reflected
- Chain Lightning
- Clumsy
- Curse
- Energy Bolt
- Explosion
- Feeblemind
- Fireball
- Flame Strike
- Harm
- Lightning
- Magic Arrow
- Mana Drain
- Mana Vampire
- Meteor Swarm
- Mind Blast
- Paralyze
- Poison
- Weaken
