# Magic Reflection
Magic Reflection bounces the next offensive spell back at its caster. It can't be recast until it has reflected a spell.

| Property | Value |
|:-|:-:|
| Circle | 5 |
| Mana | 14 |
| Duration | Until used |

## Spells Reflected
- Chain Lightning
- Clumsy
- Curse
- Energy Bolt
- Explosion
- Feeblemind
- Fireball
- Flame Strike
- Harm
- Lightning
- Magic Arrow
- Mana Drain
- Mana Vampire
- Meteor Swarm
- Mind Blast
- Paralyze
- Poison
- Weaken
