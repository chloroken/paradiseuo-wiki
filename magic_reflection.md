# Magic Reflection
Magic Reflection grants the caster a buff that reflects the next offensive spell.

**Circle**: 5
**Mana**: 14
**Duration**: Until consumed

Magic Reflection cannot be recast if it is in already in effect.

## Spells Reflected:
- Chain Lightning
- Clumsy
- Curse
- Energy Bolt
- Explosion
- Feeblemind
- Fireball
- Flame Strike
- Harm
- Lightning
- Magic Arrow
- Mana Drain
- Mana Vampire
- Meteor Swarm
- Mind Blast
- Paralyze
- Poison
- Weaken
