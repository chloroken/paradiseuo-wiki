# Summoner
The Summoner is a dedicated mage who fights alongside summoned allies, backed by powerful spells and defensive magic.

[Spirit Speak](/spirit_speak) doubles how long summons last, shields them from Dispel, and makes summoned elementals hit harder. It also lets the Summoner field all four elementals at once and makes every summon spell never fail at grandmaster. See [Summon](/summon).

Anatomy and Evaluating Intelligence combine to let the Summoner defend unarmed as if they had `110` Wrestling. See [Wrestling: Unarmed Defense](/wrestling#unarmed-defense).

Inscription strengthens [Reactive Armor](/reactive_armor) and [Protection](/protection): Reactive Armor absorbs its full `75` points, and Protection keeps a spell from being disrupted `75%` of the time. Together these make the Summoner surprisingly resilient for a caster.
