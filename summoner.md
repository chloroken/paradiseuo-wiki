# Summoner
The Summoner is a dedicated mage who fights alongside summoned allies, backed by powerful spells and defensive magic. Anatomy and Evaluating Intelligence combine to offer even more melee defense than Wrestling, and Inscription empowers Reactive Armor and Protection, making the Summoner surprisingly resilient for a caster. [Spirit Speak](/spirit_speak) doubles how long summons last, shields them from Dispel, and makes summoned elementals hit harder. It also lets the Summoner field all four elementals at once and makes every summon spell never fail. See [Summon](/summon).
