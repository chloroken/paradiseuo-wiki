# Hireling
Hirelings are townsfolk who fight for whoever pays them. They're found in inns, and once hired they follow their employer and take commands like a [pet](/pet). They're cheap enough for any character, which makes them useful company for players who adventure alone.

## Hiring
Saying `hire` near a hireling, or choosing `Hire` from its menu, makes it state its daily wage. Dropping at least one day's wage in gold on it hires it, and it says how many days the gold covers. Paying too little gets the price repeated, and the gold stays with the player.

Each day of work lasts `30` minutes of real time, and the hireling takes its wage from the gold it was paid at the start of each one. When the gold can't cover another day, it says *I quit.* and leaves, keeping its gear. Gold can't be added to a hireling that's already hired, so a longer contract means paying more up front.

Each hireling takes `2` follower slots, so a character can have at most two at once.

In Serpent's Hold and Buccaneer's Den, hirelings refuse characters who don't meet the town's Karma requirement. See [Reputation: Karma Towns](/reputation#karma-towns).

## Hirelings

| Hireling | Fights with | Wage (gold per day) |
|:-|:-:|:-:|
| Fighter | Sword or axe, shield, and armor | 7 – 10 |
| Ranger | Sword | 9 – 12 |
| Ranger archer | Bow | 9 – 12 |
| Bard | Longsword | 6 – 8 |
| Bard archer | Bow | 6 – 8 |
| Thief | Dagger | 8 – 11 |
| Sailor | Cutlass | 8 – 11 |
| Mage | Spells | 12 – 15 |
| Cleric | Healing spells | 4 – 5 |
| Beggar | Bare hands | 1 |

Each hireling's wage comes from its combat skills, so stronger hirelings cost more.

## Cleric
The cleric heals instead of fighting. It casts Heal, Greater Heal, and Cure on its employer, the employer's pets and other hirelings, and itself. Cure always comes first, and after that it heals whoever has lost the largest share of their hit points. It never heals other players or their pets.

- It only heals targets within `10` tiles that it can see, so it never wanders off to reach someone.
- It pauses `4` seconds after starting each spell, and it stops when it runs out of mana.
- A character can only have one cleric at a time, whether hired or received by transfer.
- After one cleric heals a target, other clerics can't heal that target for `10` seconds.

The cleric keeps up with light damage, but not with strong monsters or large groups.

## Commands
Hirelings take the same commands as pets, such as `all follow me`, `all guard me`, and `all kill`. They always obey. See [Pet: Controlling Pets](/pet#controlling-pets).

A hireling rides a horse while following its employer and goes on foot for any other command. The horse is only for show, and doesn't make the hireling faster.

Choosing `Dismiss` from a hireling's menu ends its contract, and any unused pay is lost. A hireling that quits or is dismissed can be hired again by anyone, and leaves for good after `3` days if no one does.

## Hirelings in Combat
Hirelings deal and take full damage against monsters, including demibosses, unlike pets. Their melee hits on other players are halved, the same as a player's.

**Hireling death is permanent.** Hirelings can't be bonded or stabled. A dead hireling's corpse holds only its gold, and its gear is lost.

## Loyalty
Hirelings lose Loyalty like pets, `10` every hour. Unless fed meat, a hireling walks off after about `10` hours, even if its pay hasn't run out. See [Pet: Loyalty](/pet#loyalty).
