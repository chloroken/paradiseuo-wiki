# Archery
Archery is the skill of fighting with bows and crossbows, which attack from a distance using arrows and bolts made with [Fletching](/fletching). Every shot uses one arrow or bolt from the backpack, hit or miss. All three weapons take both hands, so an archer can't hold a shield, and while holding one, an archer defends against an attacker's [hit chance](/combat#hit-chance) with Archery. Archers with little Magery get the same [Valor](/valor) bonuses as any other fighter, and those who hide and stealth before shooting can [ambush](/ambush) their targets for heavy damage.

## Weapons
Each weapon has its own [speed and damage](/dps). Attack speed follows the same [formula](/combat#swing-speed) as every weapon, so it slows as stamina drops.

| Weapon | Strength Required | Range (tiles) |
|:-|:-:|:-:|
| Bow | 20 | 10 |
| Crossbow | 30 | 8 |
| Heavy crossbow | 40 | 8 |

The bow and crossbow do the most damage over time. The bow reaches farthest, and the crossbow hits most steadily and is best at spreading poison. The heavy crossbow hits hardest per shot, which suits an ambush.

### Crossbow Volley
Each crossbow shot fires a volley of up to `3` bolts, as many as the archer carries. Each one uses a bolt from the backpack, and rolls its own hit, damage, and poison. Skill gain, self-poisoning, weapon wear, and a magic crossbow's damage bonus only count once per volley.

## Movement Delay
An archer has to stand still for `1` second after their last step before they can fire, and [Fletching shortens it](/fletching#movement-recovery).

## Ammunition
Arrows and bolts that hit a monster or animal have a `40%` chance to land in its backpack, where they can be looted back. Missed shots have a `40%` chance to drop on the ground, [rising with Fletching](/fletching#ammunition-recovery).

Arrows and bolts can be [coated with poison](/poisoning#poisoned-weapons), and poisoned ones are fired before clean ones. Recovered arrows and bolts are always clean.

## Manual Training
Every shot, hit or miss, has a chance to raise Archery. Attacking one's own [follower](/follower) isn't criminal, so a tamed animal makes a safe training partner, as long as there are arrows or bolts to spare.
