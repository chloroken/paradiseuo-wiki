# Shard Info & Rules
*Paradise* design aims for relaxing, nostalgic, slow, and interesting gameplay.

## Novelties
- `Stealth Archer` mechanics
- `Shiny Mount` expansion
## World
- **T2A-inspired PvM** & geography
- Enforced PvM etiquette, no bullies
- Mark, Recall, Gate disabled in Dungeons & T2A
## PvP
- **World PvP disabled**
- Stealing disabled*
- Duel Arenas enabled
- Order/Chaos (Guild PvP) enabled

^*^ ^players^ ^can^ ^still^ ^take^ ^things^ ^that^ ^were^ ^dropped^ ^to^ ^the^ ^ground^
## Skills
- Skills are mostly T2A, but not exclusively
- For more information, browse individual [skills](/Skills)
## Limits
- **Two accounts allowed** per human person
- Five characters per account ^(ten^ ^total)^
- One house per account ^(two^ ^total)^
- Young Player system enabled
## Decay
- Boat decay is 24 hours ^(to^ ^avoid^ ^boat^ ^blocking)^
- House decay is 90 days ^(to^ ^alleviate^ ^refresh^ ^pressure)^
- Full wipe every year ^(to^ ^avoid^ ^wealth^ ^inequality)^
## Macros
- **Macroing is allowed**, feel free to experiment
- Unattended macros may be used for skill training
- Macros that collect items/profit must be attended
## Admin
- **Zero tolerance** for bigotry and disruptive behavior
- Admins and GMs must never take advantage
## Development
- Progressive design, change is fundamentally good
- Closed-source development with patch notes
