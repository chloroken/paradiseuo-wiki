# Tanks
Tanks are built to stand in front of a monster and outlast it. The Blacksmith, Carpenter, and Paladin weaken a monster's hits with [one-handed maces](/weapon_effects#sapping-vigor), while the Juggernaut simply heals through them.

| Class | Unique Qualities |
|:-|:-:|
| Blacksmith | Saps a monster's vigor behind a metal shield, then splashes Crushing Blows around its target |
| Carpenter | Blocks with the sturdiest shield, then flourishes a staff into every monster around it |
| Juggernaut | Stacks food, potions, and bandages on top of Valor's toughness and regeneration |
| Paladin | Heals and cures its whole group with spells and bandages, and keeps its shield up while casting |

## Blacksmith
The Blacksmith is a self-made tank. It forges its own weapons, armor, and shields at the anvil, and [repairs](/blacksmithing#repairing) them between fights, so it never depends on anyone else for gear. Its whole kit is metal, and that matters: plate and shields forged from [colored metal](/armor#metal-types) ward off spell damage, up to a tenth of it for a full valorite set with a shield. It forges and repairs that colored metal itself, so it's always ready for casters, such as liches and the Lich King.

In a fight, it holds the front with a one-handed mace and a metal shield. Every mace hit, powered by [Arms Lore](/arms_lore), saps the monster's vigor, so its melee hits land as if it were weaker. Blacksmithing makes its mace [hit harder](/weapons#scaling-skills), so each hit also saps more. Shield blocks, [Mace Fighting](/mace_fighting), and [Valor](/valor)'s accuracy and toughness keep the damage that does land low. When monsters bunch up, it switches to a war hammer or war axe. With Arms Lore behind it, a two-handed mace lands Crushing Blows that halve a monster's attack speed, and Blacksmithing makes each one [splash](/weapon_effects#crushing-blow) the same hit onto every monster around the target.

It heals with potions it brews itself. [Alchemy](/alchemy#healing) more than doubles what a heal potion does for it, and a potion never slips the way a bandage does, which suits a tank that's hit constantly. Alchemy also makes its [explosion potions](/alchemy#explosion) hit harder, for monsters too spread out to splash.

Its limits come from what it leaves out. Without [Anatomy](/anatomy), it gets no Valor regeneration, and between potions it has nothing to heal with. Every potion costs reagents. It has no ranged attack and no magic, so it walks everywhere and has to close the distance to anything it fights.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `100` Dexterity, `25` Intelligence
- Skills: Blacksmithing, Mace Fighting, [Tactics](/tactics), Arms Lore, Parrying, Alchemy, and Resisting Spells
- Gear: one-handed mace, metal shield, and a war hammer or war axe, with colored metal plate
- Defense: Mace Fighting, shield blocks, Valor's accuracy and toughness, colored metal, and resisted spells
- Healing: stronger heal potions
- Weakness: no Anatomy or Healing, so no Valor regeneration and nothing to heal with between potions

## Carpenter
The Carpenter is a tank built around wood. It crafts its own clubs, staves, and wooden shields, and [Carpentry](/carpentry) makes a wooden shield the [sturdiest shield](/parrying#shields) in the game against weapons, with more armor than even a chaos shield. That makes it the best tank against monsters that hit hard in melee.

It tanks with a club and that wooden shield. A club is a one-handed mace, so with Arms Lore it saps a monster's vigor, and Carpentry makes it hit harder and sap more. When it's surrounded, it switches to a black, gnarled, or quarter staff, which Carpentry also strengthens. A staff's Crushing Blow slows its target, and Carpentry lets it flourish, sweeping the same hit through every monster standing next to the Carpenter, so it rewards being mobbed.

Wood can't be forged from colored metal, so its shield never wards off spells, and casters hurt it more than tanks in colored metal. It heals with its own stronger potions and has no Anatomy, so it gets no Valor regeneration. A staff is two-handed, so flourishing means giving up the shield.

## Juggernaut
The Juggernaut is the immovable object. It gives up Tactics, and with it most of its damage, to stack three separate layers of healing on top of a shield, Valor's toughness, and Valor's five-fold regeneration. It isn't trying to kill quickly, only to still be standing when everything else has fallen.

Its healing layers each work on their own timer. Bandages heal steadily, and Anatomy lets them cure poison. Alchemy more than doubles what a heal potion does for it, so a potion gives a large, reliable heal every `10` seconds. Cooking turns [food](/cooking#eating-as-a-cook) into an emergency button: a few bites of savory food can restore its whole health bar in seconds. The price is hunger. Once its stomach is full, it has to wait for hunger to drop, or drink [alcohol](/cooking#alcohol) between fights, before it can eat for healing again.

It fights with a kryss or war fork and a shield, so it needs no Arms Lore, and wears plate, ideally colored metal. Without Tactics, it does about a third of the damage of other fighters and misses more often, so solo fights take a long time. It shines in groups, holding a demiboss while others do the damage. Taking Tactics instead of Resisting Spells triples its damage and adds Valor's accuracy, which makes it far better alone, but casters then hurt it badly.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `100` Dexterity, `25` Intelligence
- Skills: Fencing, Anatomy, Parrying, Healing, Alchemy, Cooking, and Resisting Spells
- Gear: kryss or war fork and shield, with plate
- Defense: Fencing, shield blocks, Valor's toughness, and resisted spells
- Healing: food for bursts, stronger potions in between, bandages for steady healing, and Valor's regeneration
- Weakness: no Tactics, so it does about a third of the damage of other fighters and misses more often

## Paladin
The Paladin is a holy knight: a mace-and-shield tank with just enough [Magery](/magery) to heal and cure the whole group. It weakens monsters' hits with a one-handed mace like the other tanks, and its `60` Anatomy lets its [bandages](/healing) cure poison. With only `40` Magery, it keeps most of Valor: three fifths of the accuracy and toughness a pure fighter gets, and some of the regeneration.

Its shield stays on while it casts, so it can hold the front and still look after everyone behind it. Heal and Cure reach allies at range, and its bandages heal and cure anyone beside it. It fights in metal, so it can wear colored metal plate and a colored metal shield for protection from spells. It's the tank a group wants when the damage is spread around.

Its catch is its mace. Casting still puts the mace in the backpack, so every spell leaves it fighting and defending with bare hands until it re-equips. Its low Magery keeps it to the lowest circles, so its heals are small next to a mage's.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `65` Dexterity, `60` Intelligence
- Skills: Mace Fighting, Arms Lore, Healing, Parrying, Tactics, Resisting Spells, `40` Magery, and `60` Anatomy
- Gear: one-handed mace, shield, and spellbook, with plate
- Defense: Mace Fighting, shield blocks, most of Valor, colored metal, and resisted spells
- Healing: bandages and spells, for itself and its group
- Weakness: casting puts its mace in the backpack, so it must time its spells wisely
