# Magic Items
Jewelry and clothing found as loot can hold a magic effect, which works while the item is worn and spends charges as it goes. Magic items always drop unidentified, and single-clicking one shows only that until it's identified with [Item Identification](/item_identification). Once identified, single-clicking shows the effect and the charges left, such as *Ring [Bless: 12]*. When the last charge is spent, *The magic in your ring fades.* and it becomes an ordinary ring. Magic items can't be crafted or recharged. Magic weapons and armor are covered on [Magic Weapons](/magic_weapons) and [Armor](/armor).

## Effects
Each effect works like the spell it's named after. Effects that last keep themselves going while the item is worn, spending a charge each time they're renewed. Agility, Cunning, Strength, and Bless don't spend charges while a spell already gives at least as large a bonus.

| Effect | Charges | While Worn |
|:-|:-:|:-:|
| [Agility](/agility) | 10 – 30 | Dexterity +10, renewed every 2 minutes |
| [Cunning](/cunning) | 10 – 30 | Intelligence +10, renewed every 2 minutes |
| [Strength](/strength) | 10 – 30 | Strength +10, renewed every 2 minutes |
| [Bless](/bless) | 5 – 20 | Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence +10, renewed every 2 minutes |
| [Protection](/protection) | 10 – 30 | The strongest Protection, renewed every 4 minutes |
| [Night Sight](/night_sight) | 10 – 40 | Night sight, renewed when it wears off |
| [Magic Reflection](/magic_reflection) | 20 – 60 | Magic Reflection, using a charge every 5 seconds |
| [Invisibility](/invisibility) | 5 – 25 | Equipping it turns the wearer invisible for up to 2 minutes |
| [Teleport](/teleport) | 5 – 15 | Double-clicking the ring teleports up to 12 tiles |

The bonuses from Agility, Cunning, Strength, and Bless stay for the rest of their `2` minutes after the item comes off. Invisibility spends one charge each time the item is put on. Teleport is only found on rings, and follows the same rules as the spell.

### Cursed Jewelry
Some jewelry is cursed instead. A cursed item lowers its wearer's stats while worn, renewing the curse every `2` minutes, and the curse lingers for up to `2` minutes after the item comes off. Cursed items have `10` to `30` charges, and clothing is never cursed.

| Curse | While Worn |
|:-|:-:|
| [Clumsy](/clumsy) | Dexterity -10 |
| [Feeblemind](/feeblemind) | Intelligence -10 |
| [Weaken](/weaken) | Strength -10 |
| [Curse](/curse) | Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence -10 |

## Finding Magic Items
A magic item is equally likely to be jewelry or clothing. Most sources give ordinary magic items. Strong sources give a better mix, with Invisibility twice as common and Magic Reflection and Teleport rings found only there.

| Effect | Ordinary Weight | Strong Weight |
|:-|:-:|:-:|
| Agility, Cunning, Strength, Bless, Protection, and Night Sight | 2 each | 2 each |
| Invisibility | 2 | 4 |
| Magic Reflection | 0 | 2 |
| Teleport (rings only) | 0 | 2 |
| Each curse (jewelry only) | 1 | 1 |

An item's chance of an effect is that effect's weight out of the total for its kind. Ordinary clothing, for example, has `14` weight in all, so each effect has a `1` in `7` chance.

Strong magic items come from [demibosses](/demibosses), level `4` and `5` [treasure chests](/treasure_map#loot), and level `4` [dungeon chests](/lockpicking#dungeon-chests). Ordinary ones come from lower-level chests and the richest monsters.

## Wands
Wands are found alongside magic items, a little more often. A wand must be held to use it. Double-clicking it casts its spell instantly, with no mana, reagents, or chance to fail, and spends a charge. Afterward, every wand the user carries needs `4` seconds to recharge. Wands drop unidentified too.

| Wand | Charges |
|:-|:-:|
| Clumsy | 5 – 30 |
| Feeblemind | 5 – 30 |
| Weaken | 5 – 30 |
| Mana Drain | 5 – 30 |
| Magic Arrow | 5 – 30 |
| Harm | 5 – 30 |
| Heal | 10 – 25 |
| Fireball | 5 – 15 |
| Lightning | 5 – 20 |
| Greater Heal | 1 – 5 |
| Identification | 25 – 175 |

Every kind of wand is equally likely. A wand of identification identifies any magic item, wand, weapon, or piece of armor up to `6` tiles away.

## Monster Drops
Each monster's loot comes in shares, and some monsters roll a share more than once. A dragon rolls two filthy rich shares, for example, so it has two chances at each.

| Share | Example Monsters | Magic Item (%) | Wand (%) |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Average | Trolls, ogres, gazers | 0 | 2 |
| Rich | Liches, daemons, drakes, cyclopes | 0 | 5 |
| Filthy rich | Dragons, balrons, lich lords, elder gazers, titans | 10 | 10 |

Demibosses also drop strong magic items and wands of their own, listed in each demiboss's loot.
