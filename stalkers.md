# Stalkers
Stalkers use [Hiding](/hiding) and [Stealth](/stealth) to get into position, then strike before the enemy can answer. With `100` Hiding and `100` Stealth, all four can [vanish](/stealth#vanish) when a fight turns against them and walk away.

| Class | Unique Qualities |
|:-|:-:|
| Hunter | Snipes from stealth with a heavy crossbow, then keeps shooting poisoned volleys |
| Puppetmaster | Sends golems in from hiding, then picks and disarms the chests |
| Rogue | Backstabs from stealth, then picks the chests its victims guarded |
| Thief | Robs monsters and townsfolk without a fight, then picks and disarms the chests |

## Hunter
The Hunter is a patient sniper. [Tracking](/tracking) finds its prey, and it stalks it unseen and opens with a heavy crossbow [ambush](/ambush). After ten stealthed steps, the ambush does two and a half times damage and can't miss, the biggest single hit of any weapon in the game.

Whatever survives the opening shot gets the crossbow. Each shot fires a [volley](/archery#crossbow-volley) of bolts poisoned with [Poisoning](/poisoning), and Tracking makes them land more often, so poison keeps ticking while the target closes in. Its [Anatomy](/anatomy) adds damage and [Valor](/valor)'s regeneration. When something reaches it, the Hunter vanishes, walks off, and lines up a shot on a fresh target.

A monster stays alert for a minute after an ambush, so the Hunter gets one big opener per target. Stealth limits it to leather, and it has no [Parrying](/parrying), no melee skill, and no healing skill, so a long, straight fight wears it down quickly.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `100` Dexterity, `25` Intelligence
- Skills: Archery, Tactics, Anatomy, Poisoning, Tracking, Hiding, and Stealth
- Gear: heavy crossbow and crossbow, with leather
- Defense: range, stealth, vanishing, and Valor's accuracy and regeneration
- Healing: unbonused potions
- Weakness: stealth limits it to light armor, and it has no melee skill or healing skill

## Puppetmaster
The Puppetmaster is a dungeon lockpicker that never lifts a weapon. It builds [golems](/golem) with [Tinkering](/tinkering) and brings them to life with [Inscription](/inscription), sneaks up to a chest's guards, and lets its machines do the fighting. Once the room is clear, it picks the lock, disarms the chest's trap with [Remove Trap](/remove_trap), and takes the loot.

Its golems are invulnerable, attack the nearest monster on their own, and are ignored by monsters. Unleashing one doesn't reveal the Puppetmaster, so it can stay hidden the whole time they work. A terror golem lasts `30` seconds, and another can be unleashed as soon as it winds down, so with two golems one is always fighting. Each usually drops back to the ground to be picked up again. If something does spot the Puppetmaster, it eats for a quick burst of health with [Cooking](/cooking#eating-as-a-cook) and vanishes. That makes it a relaxed, low-stress way to delve dungeons, well suited to casual players.

Golems are weak next to a real fighter, and the Puppetmaster can't fight on its own at all. Each golem costs gems and a Blade Spirits scroll, which it has to buy since it has no [Magery](/magery) to scribe them, and now and then a golem breaks down for good. Its food heals in bursts rather than steadily, and it can only cure poison with potions.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `100` Dexterity, `25` Intelligence
- Skills: Tinkering, Inscription, Hiding, Stealth, Lockpicking, Remove Trap, and Cooking
- Gear: golems, lockpicks, Blade Spirits scrolls, and food, with leather
- Defense: stealth, vanishing, and golems that do the fighting
- Healing: food
- Weakness: golems are weak and cost gems, and it can't fight on its own

## Rogue
The Rogue is a backstabber and a chest robber. It creeps up behind its target and opens with a poisoned dagger, where ten stealthed steps make the ambush do five times damage and never miss. Then it finishes the fight quickly with a kryss or war fork and picks the [dungeon chests](/lockpicking#dungeon-chests) its victims were guarding.

A Rogue's fights are short by design. The backstab does most of the work and poison keeps chipping away even when the Rogue runs off. Lockpicking and Remove Trap turn every cleared room into loot, since dungeon chests hold gold, gems, magic items, and [wands](/magic_items#wands), and get richer the deeper they're found.

It's fragile once it has to stand and fight. Leather, no shield, no Parrying, and healing that comes only from potions mean a Rogue that loses the element of surprise should leave, which is what vanishing is for.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `100` Dexterity, `25` Intelligence
- Skills: Fencing, Tactics, Hiding, Stealth, Poisoning, Lockpicking, and Remove Trap
- Gear: daggers, krysses, and war forks, with leather
- Defense: stealth, vanishing, Valor's accuracy, and Fencing
- Healing: unbonused potions
- Weakness: fragile once it has to stand and fight, with no shield and potions only, so it leans on vanishing to escape

## Thief
The Thief never draws a weapon. It slips into dungeons and towns unseen and [steals](/stealing) from the pockets of monsters and townsfolk, where every pocket holds a little loot and a chance at a lottery prize, from a pouch of gems to a castle deed. [Snooping](/snooping) guides its hand straight to the prize, and [Lockpicking](/lockpicking) and [Remove Trap](/remove_trap) open the chests along the way.

A Thief that isn't caught stays hidden the whole time, so a careful one can rob a whole room without a fight. When a monster does catch it, the Thief [begs for mercy](/begging#begging-for-mercy) to calm it and keeps working, or vanishes and walks away. In town, getting caught means a short stay in [jail](/jail), which it can pick its way out of. In the [Karma towns](/stealing#karma-towns), it costs Karma instead, which makes them the riskiest and richest pockets of all.

It can't fight at all, so a Thief that's out of escapes is in real trouble. Monsters that can see hidden players, and two monsters catching it at once, are its worst nightmares.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `100` Dexterity, `25` Intelligence
- Skills: Stealing, Snooping, Begging, Hiding, Stealth, Lockpicking, and Remove Trap
- Gear: lockpicks and potions, with leather or no armor, and empty hands for stealing
- Defense: stealth, vanishing, and begging for mercy
- Healing: unbonused potions
- Weakness: no way to fight, so it depends entirely on not being caught and on its escapes
