# Druid
The Druid is a tamer who combines animal companions with offensive and defensive magic. Animal Lore and Animal Taming allow it to control powerful creatures. With Evaluating Intelligence, the Druid can hit hard with spells when necessary. With 40 Dexterity, the Druid reaches the 4-second bandage breakpoint for Veterinary to keep pets alive.

## Skills
- 100 Animal Taming
- 100 Animal Lore
- 100 Veterinary
- 100 Magery
- 100 Evaluating Intelligence
- 100 Meditation
- 100 Resisting Spells or Wrestling

## Stats
| Stat | Amount |
|:-|:-:|
| Strength | 100 |
