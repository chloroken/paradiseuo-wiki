# Shadow
The Shadow is a stealthy archer who specializes in ambushes. Hiding and Stealth allow the Shadow to approach targets undetected before delivering a devastating opening shot. Without Magery, the Shadow receives the full Valor accuracy and regeneration bonuses. The Shadow prefers a heavy crossbow to take advantage of the increased damage from an ambush, but can switch to a bow for higher sustained damage after the opener.

## Skills
- 100 Archery
- 100 Tactics
- 100 Anatomy
- 100 Hiding
- 100 Stealth
- 100 Healing
- 100 Resisting Spells or Lockpicking

Lockpicking can replace Resisting Spells to allow access to dungeon chests, which fits the theme.
