# Monk
The Monk combines unarmed combat, stuns, light armor, and offensive magic. Evaluating Intelligence makes spells hit hard, tactics increases unarmed damage, high Wrestling and Anatomy give its punches a chance to stun opponents. See [Wrestling Moves](/wrestling#wrestling-moves).

## Skills
- 100 Magery
- 100 Evaluating Intelligence
- 100 Meditation
- 100 Wrestling
- 100 Anatomy
- 100 Tactics
- 100 Resisting Spells
