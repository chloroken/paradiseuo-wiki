# Treasure Map
`Treasure Maps` drop from monsters and have a level from `0` to `5`, with stronger monsters dropping higher-level maps. Level `0` maps only drop for `Young` players.

A map must be decoded with [Cartography](/Cartography) before finding the depicted location in the world. Then the `Treasure Chest` must then be dug up with a shovel (made easier with [Mining](/Mining)), unlocked with [Lockpicking](/Lockpicking), its trap disarmed with [Magery](/Magery) or [Remove Trap](/Remove_Trap), and finally the guardian monsters defeated. Only then does the `Treasure Chest` become vulnerable.

However, monsters continue spawning as the chest is looted, so be vigilant. The type of monsters that spawn are tied to the level of the `Treasure Chest`.
## Decoding
Double click a `Treasure Map` to try decoding it.
| Map Level | Minimum | Guaranteed | Gain Limit |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 0 | 0 Cartography | 0 Cartography | 0 Cartography |
| 1 | 0 Cartography | 0 Cartography | 30 Cartography |
| 2 | 50 Cartography | 60 Cartography | 90 Cartography |
| 3 | 65 Cartography | 75 Cartography | Never |
| 4 | 80 Cartography | 90 Cartography | Never |
| 5 | 90 Cartography | 100 Cartography | Never |
## Lockpicking
A [Lockpicker](/Lockpicker) can use a [lockpick](/Lockpick) on a `Treasure Chest` to try opening it.
## Disarming
The `Treasure Chest` can be disarmed by [Telekinesis](/Spells/Telekinesis) at a range to avoid the explosion and thus the damage. Without [Magery](/Skills/Magery), one would need to use [Remove Trap](/Skills/Remove_Trap) or simply eat the trap damage.
## Loot
The loot dropped by a `Treasure Chest` is tied to its `Treasure Map` level.
* `Treasure Map` drops are always one level lower
| Chance | Drop | Amount |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 100% | `Gold` | 5,000 × level |
| 100% | `Reagents` | (40 ~ 60) × (level + 1) |
| 100%| `Gems` | 3 × level + 1 |
| 100% | `Magic Equipment` | 6 × level |
| 100% | `Scrolls` | 5 × level |
| 50% | `Treasure Map` | 1 |
| 1% | [Skeleton Key](/Items/Skeleton_Key) | 1 |
