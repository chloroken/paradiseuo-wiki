# Demibosses
Demibosses are powerful monsters unique to *Paradise*, built for groups of players. Each one rules its own lair, returns hours after it dies, and drops far more loot than an ordinary monster, including an artifact found nowhere else. They exist to give groups a reason to gather: a fight that takes planning and a mix of classes, with a reward worth sharing.

Demibosses don't always follow the rules of other [monsters](/monsters). They walk around obstacles to reach their target, and their melee hits and most of their spells do double damage to followers.

## Demibosses

|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| [Lich King](/lich_king) | Covetous, floor 3 | 9,000 – 11,000 | 12 – 24 | [Liche's ring](/lich_king#liches-ring) | 10 |
| [Greater Elemental](/greater_elemental) | Shame, floor 4 | 20,000 – 30,000 | 12 – 24 | [Primal amulet](/greater_elemental#primal-amulet) | 50 |
| [Forgotten One](/forgotten_one) | Deceit, floor 3 | 15,000 – 20,000 | 12 – 24 | [Spiteful memory](/forgotten_one#spiteful-memory) | 10 |

- A liche's ring turns any weapon silver, at the cost of its user's life.
- A primal amulet makes one elemental summon spell cost no mana or reagents, once every `10` seconds.
- Spiteful memory drains a lich's or lich lord's mana on a weapon hit, once every `5` minutes, but crumbles if a lich or lich lord kills its owner.

## Strategy
The Lich King and the Greater Elemental fight mainly with spells, so the strongest tools against them are the ones that stop a caster or starve it of mana. Effects that only weaken melee attacks matter less against them. The Forgotten One is the opposite: he has no mana and fights only in melee.

### Weapon Effects
- [Concussion Blow](/weapon_effects#concussion-blow) from polearms halves a boss's Intelligence, and with it its maximum mana, for `30` seconds. The Lich King drops to `400` to `500` mana, and the Greater Elemental to `200` to `225`.
- [Paralyzing Blow](/weapon_effects#paralyzing-blow) from spears and wrestling [stuns](/weapon_effects#stun) freeze the boss. A frozen boss can't start a new spell, though it finishes one already being cast. A frozen Lich King can't teleport either, but the Greater Elemental still teleports and summons.
- [Silver](/magic_weapons#silver) weapons do double damage to the Lich King, who is undead. He drops one himself.
- An [ambush](/ambush) opens the fight with up to `2.5×` damage, and since fights last a while, multiple ambushes are possible.
- Sapping vigor and Crushing Blow only weaken melee attacks. The Lich King and the Greater Elemental hardly rely on melee, but the Forgotten One relies on nothing else.

### Magic
- The Lich King's `150` to `200` [Resisting Spells](/resisting_spells) blunts damage spells. A grandmaster mage does a little over half the damage to him that they do to the Greater Elemental, so weapons do most of the work against him. See [Evaluating Intelligence: Spell Damage](/evaluating_intelligence#spell-damage).
- Mana Vampire steals as much of a boss's mana as the caster is missing. At `100` Magery, the Greater Elemental resists it about a quarter of the time, and the Lich King half to three quarters of the time. After a Concussion Blow, a few casts can empty a boss.
- Paralyze stops a boss from casting until damage breaks it, which buys time to heal and cure. Any hit ends it, so the group has to hold back.
- [Poison](/poison) works on the Greater Elemental, but not on the Lich King or the Forgotten One. The Greater Elemental can cure itself, but every Cure is a spell it isn't casting at the group.

### Bard Skills
- Demibosses have far too many hit points for any bard. Provocation, Peacemaking, and Discordance can't affect them.
- Peacemaking aimed at the bard still pacifies everything nearby, bosses included, for `1` second.
- Bards are best used on the adds: [provoking](/provocation) the Greater Elemental's elementals into each other, especially the blood and poison elementals that appear below half health, and turning the Lich King's skeletons on each other.

### Tamers and Followers
- Followers take double damage from a demiboss, so pets, hirelings, and summons should fight the adds, not the boss. See [Follower: Followers in Combat](/follower#followers-in-combat).
- The Greater Elemental's adds vanish after `60` seconds and return at its next teleport, so killing them only buys time. Keeping them busy with pets works better than killing them repeatedly.

### Lich King
- A player bound by Blood Oath should stop attacking him until it ends, since every hit they land also hits them.
- Strangle does more damage as its target's stamina drops, so its target should keep their stamina up.
- Once hurt, he sometimes casts a heal on himself in combat, at most once every `10` to `20` seconds. A frozen boss can't cast, so freezes can interrupt those heals.
- He takes items from corpses within `2` tiles, so the fight should move away from anyone who falls.
- He blinks back to his throne when he's more than `8` tiles from it, and his skeletons return within `30` seconds, so the fight always stays in the throne room.

### Greater Elemental
- It never heals itself, so steady damage over a long fight wins.
- Below half health, blood and poison elementals join its summons and it teleports twice as often. Groups should save cure potions and provocation for that phase.

### Forgotten One
- Only a tank can hold him. He is too fast to kite, so ranged characters and casters can't fight him on their own.
- His hits are weak but constant, so defenses that reduce every hit work exceptionally well, such as shields and [Reactive Armor](/reactive_armor). See [Armor: How Armor Works](/armor#how-armor-works).
- He never switches targets on his own, so one tank can hold him and pass him to a second tank when they need to cure and heal.
- Concussion Blow does nothing to him, since he has no mana.
- Followers can draw his attention, but they can't tank him. They take double damage and have no way to reduce his hits.
