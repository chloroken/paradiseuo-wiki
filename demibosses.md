# Demibosses
Demibosses are powerful monsters found in [dungeons](/dungeons), built for groups of players but *conceivably* soloable. Each one rules its own lair, returns hours after it dies, and drops far more loot than an ordinary monster, including an artifact found nowhere else. They exist to give groups a reason to gather: a fight that takes planning and a mix of classes, with a reward worth sharing.

Demibosses don't always follow the rules of other [monsters](/monsters). They walk around obstacles to reach their target, and their melee hits and most of their spells do double damage to followers.

## Demibosses
| Demiboss | Location | Hit Points | Respawn (hours) | Artifact | Chance (%) |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| [Lich King](/lich_king) | Covetous | 9,000 – 11,000 | 12 – 24 | [Liche's ring](/lich_king#liches-ring) | 10 |

## Effects on Demibosses
How the effects players can use work on each demiboss.

| Effect | Source | Lich King | Greater Elemental | Forgotten One |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| [Concussion Blow](/weapon_effects#concussion-blow) | Polearms | Mana drops to 400 – 500 for 30 seconds | Mana drops to 200 – 225 for 30 seconds | No effect |
| [Paralyzing Blow](/weapon_effects#paralyzing-blow) and [stuns](/weapon_effects#stun) | Spears and wrestling | Frozen, so he can't start a spell or teleport | Frozen, so it can't start a spell, but it still teleports and summons | Frozen |
| Sapping vigor | One-handed maces | Weaker melee hits | Weaker melee hits | Weaker hits |
| Crushing Blow | Two-handed maces and staves | Slower melee attacks | Slower melee attacks | Slower attacks |
| [Silver](/magic_weapons#silver) | Silver weapons | Double damage | No effect | Double damage |
| Damage spells | Magery | Resisting Spells 150 – 200 | Resisting Spells 100 – 110 | No Resisting Spells |
| Mana Vampire | Magery | Resisted half to three quarters of the time at 100 Magery | Resisted about a quarter of the time at 100 Magery | No effect |
| Paralyze | Magery | Can't move or cast until damage breaks it | Can't move or cast until damage breaks it | Can't move until damage breaks it, but still attacks anything in reach |
| [Poison](/poison) | Poisoned weapons and spells | Immune | Poisoned, but it can cure itself | Immune |
| Provocation, Peacemaking, and Discordance | Bard skills | No effect | No effect | No effect |

- Concussion Blow and Mana Vampire do nothing to the Forgotten One, who has no mana.
- Sapping vigor and Crushing Blow only affect melee. The Lich King and the Greater Elemental fight mainly with spells, but the Forgotten One fights only in melee.
- Paralyze ends at the first hit, from anyone.
- Peacemaking aimed at the bard does pacify demibosses for `1` second.
- The Lich King drops a silver weapon himself, listed in his [loot](/lich_king#loot).
