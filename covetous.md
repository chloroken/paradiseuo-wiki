# Covetous
Covetous is a [dungeon](/dungeons) west of [Vesper](/towns) with three floors joined by two outdoor mountain passes. Its [monsters](/monsters) grow tougher on each floor, from the harpies and gazers of floors 1 and 2 to the undead masses of floor 3, ruled by the [Lich King](/lich_king).

## History
Covetous was hewn by slaves as a crypt for the ancient rulers of the lands that became Minoc, and it is bound to the virtue of [Sacrifice](https://wiki.ultimacodex.com/wiki/Sacrifice). The Avatar once braved its depths for the [Orange Stone of Sacrifice](https://wiki.ultimacodex.com/wiki/Virtue_Stones). In time it was dug out as a mine, and its miners found [blackrock](https://wiki.ultimacodex.com/wiki/Blackrock), a stone that smothers magic. Ages later the mine was abandoned, and the liche [Khelereth](https://wiki.ultimacodex.com/wiki/Khelereth) claimed it with his undead. Before he was struck down, he had steeped himself in blackrock and buried his phylactery deep within the mountain soil. Years later, when Khelereth was all but forgotten, an unnamed liche stained in black took up rule within Covetous once again. Is it a coincidence, or could it be him?

## Floor 1
Floor 1 has harpies, stone harpies, headless ones, gazers, gazer larvae, corpsers, and skeletons.

## Mountain Pass A
Headless ones and harpies guard the pass between floors 1 and 2.

## Floor 2
Floor 2 has the same monsters as floor 1, along with elder gazers, slimes, water elementals, giant spiders, and dread spiders.

## Mountain Pass B
Skeletons, bone knights, and stone harpies guard the pass between floors 2 and 3.

The Lich King's dark magic prevents [Mark](/mark) and [Gate Travel](/gate_travel) in this area. [Recall](/recall) may only be used to leave.

## Floor 3
Floor 3 is an undead necropolis carved into the mountain. Skeletons, bone knights, skeletal knights, shades, spectres, wraiths, mummies, zombies, and liches can be found throughout. In the back of the main area, there are stairs that lead to an undead prison, which houses magical undead and a lich lord. Behind the throne room, another lich lord guards stairs that lead to a cave with drakes and a dragon. The Lich King sits in the throne room surrounded by his army of skeletons.

## Curiosities

### Warning Statues
Statues in the necropolis on floor 3 warn those who draw near.

### Necropolis Switches
Five switches stand in the necropolis on floor 3. They seem to respond to something, and the throne room may hold a clue.
