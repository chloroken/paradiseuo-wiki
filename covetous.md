# Covetous
Covetous is a dungeon west of Vesper with three floors joined by two mountain passes. Its monsters grow tougher on each floor, from harpies and gazers near the entrance to the undead of floor 3 and their ruler, the Lich King.

## Floor 1
Floor 1 has harpies, stone harpies, headless ones, gazers, gazer larvae, and corpsers. Monsters fill even the dead-end hallways.

## Mountain Pass A
Headless ones and harpies guard the pass between floors 1 and 2.

## Floor 2
Floor 2 has the same monsters as floor 1, along with elder gazers, slimes, water elementals, giant spiders, and dread spiders. The dread spiders keep to the back of the caves.

## Mountain Pass B
Skeletons, bone knights, and stone harpies guard the pass between floors 2 and 3.

## Floor 3
Floor 3 belongs to the undead: skeletons, bone knights, shades, spectres, mummies, zombies, liches can be found throughout. In the back of the main area, there are stairs that lead to an undead prison, which houses magical undead. Behind the throne room, there are stairs that lead to a dragon cave. Both areas are guarded by a lich lord.

The throne room holds the Lich King.

## Lich King
The Lich King is a black lich who rules from the throne room on floor 3. He is built for a group of players, though a strong player may be able to defeat him alone. After he dies, he returns `12` to `24` hours later.

The throne room raises skeletons throughout the fight, a new one every `15` to `30` seconds from each of its two spawn points, up to `10` at a time.

### Fighting the Lich King
- He has `5,000` to `6,000` hit points.
- He casts both Magery and Necromancy spells. Players can't learn Necromancy, so these are spells players won't see anywhere else.
- His [Resisting Spells](/resisting_spells) is `150` to `200`, so he resists spells far more often than other monsters.
- Poison has no effect on him.
- He is undead, so [silver](/weapon#silver) weapons do double damage to him.

**The Lich King takes items from corpses near him, including fallen players.** He takes one item at a time from a corpse within `2` tiles, showing *\*rummages through a corpse and takes an item\**. Taken items drop with his own loot when he dies.

### Loot
The Lich King drops:
- a large hoard of gold and magic items
- `10` high-level spell scrolls
- a guaranteed level `5` [treasure map](/treasure_map)
- a guaranteed silver weapon
