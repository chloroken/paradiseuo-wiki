# Covetous
Covetous is a dungeon west of Vesper with three floors joined by two mountain passes. Its monsters grow tougher on each floor, from harpies and gazers near the entrance to the undead of floor 3 and their ruler, the [Lich King](/lich_king).

## Lore
Covetous was hewn by slaves as a crypt for the ancient rulers of the lands that became Minoc, and it is bound to the virtue of Sacrifice. In time it was dug out as a mine, and its miners found blackrock, a stone that smothers magic. Legends from other Britannias tell of the liche Khelereth, who claimed a Covetous of his own and was struck down there, steeped in blackrock. Now, in our Britannia, a mysterious Lich King rules Covetous's throne room, his body blackened by the very same stone.

## Floor 1
Floor 1 has harpies, stone harpies, headless ones, gazers, gazer larvae, and corpsers.

## Mountain Pass A
Headless ones and harpies guard the pass between floors 1 and 2.

## Floor 2
Floor 2 has the same monsters as floor 1, along with elder gazers, slimes, water elementals, giant spiders, and dread spiders.

## Mountain Pass B
Skeletons, bone knights, and stone harpies guard the pass between floors 2 and 3.

The Lich King's dark magic prevents Mark and Gate Travel in this area. Recall may only be used to leave.

## Floor 3
Floor 3 is an undead fortress carved into the mountain. Skeletons, bone knights, shades, spectres, mummies, zombies, and liches can be found throughout. In the back of the main area, there are stairs that lead to an undead prison, which houses magical undead and a lich lord. Behind the throne room, another lich lord guards stairs that lead to a cave with drakes and a dragon. The Lich King sits in the throne room surrounded by his army of skeletons.
