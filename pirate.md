# Pirate
The Pirate is a self-sufficient treasure hunter who handles every stage of treasure hunting alone. It farms maps with Provocation, decodes them with Cartography, travels to their locations with Magery, picks chests with Lockpicking, disarms traps with Telekinesis, and provokes the guardians against each other. Although not required, it would be a terrible shame if the Pirate didn't have a boat.

## Skills
- 100 Magery
- 100 Meditation
- 100 Musicianship
- 100 Provocation
- 100 Cartography
- 100 Lockpicking
- 100 Hiding
