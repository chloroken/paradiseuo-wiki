# Mining
Mining is the skill of digging ore out of mountainsides and cave floors with a pickaxe or shovel, then smelting it into ingots at a forge for [Blacksmithing](/blacksmithing) and [Tinkering](/tinkering). Provisioners in every town sell shovels, and tinkers can make both tools. Miners can't dig while mounted or polymorphed, and must stand within `2` tiles of the rock. Mining also makes the [pickaxe](/weapons#scaling-skills) hit harder, and makes it easier to dig up a [treasure chest](/treasure_map).

## Ore and Veins
The world is divided into squares `8` tiles across, and each square holds one vein of ore. A vein's metal never changes, so a miner who finds a colored vein can come back to it. Each vein holds `10` to `34` ore and refills `10` to `20` minutes after it's first dug. A successful dig brings up `2` ore.

| Copper | 75 | 8.4 | 81 | 100 |

| Valorite | 99 | 1.4 | 51 | 52 |

Half of the digs in a colored vein bring up iron instead. A miner without enough Mining for a vein's metal only ever finds iron there. Metal type matters for armor, since colored metal [wards off spells](/armor#metal-types).

## Smelting
Double-clicking ore and targeting a forge within `2` tiles smelts it into ingots, even while the ore is still in a pack animal's backpack. Colored ore needs the same Mining to smelt as to dig. The chance rises evenly from `25` below that requirement until it never fails `25` above it, with iron counted as `50`. A failed smelt burns away half the ore.

Ore comes in piles of different sizes. Most are large piles, which smelt into `2` ingots each. Medium piles smelt into `1` ingot each, and small piles take `2` ore for each ingot. Targeting another pile of the same metal instead of a forge combines the two.

## Manual Training
Mining trains on every dig, from the first swing at `0` all the way to grandmaster on iron. Once colored veins open up past `65`, mining them trains just as well and pays far better.
