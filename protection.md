# Protection
Protection grants the caster bonus armor that doesn't affect Meditation.

**Circle**: 2
**Mana**: 6
**Armor**: `(EvalInt + Meditation + Inscription) / 4`
**Minimum Duration**: 15 seconds
**Maximum Duration**:`Magery × 2` seconds

Protection cannot be recast if it is in already in effect.
