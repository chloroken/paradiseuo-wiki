# Protection
Protection gives the caster bonus armor that doesn't interfere with Meditation. It can't be recast while it's already in effect.

| Property | Value |
|:-|:-:|
| Circle | 2 |
| Mana | 6 |
| Armor | (Evaluating Intelligence + Meditation + Inscription) / 4 |
| Minimum Duration (s) | 15 |
| Maximum Duration (s) | Magery * 2 |
