# Protection
Protection keeps the caster's spells from being disrupted. While it's active, each hit taken while casting has a chance not to disrupt the spell, but every spell takes longer to cast. It can't be recast while active.

| Property | Value |
|:-|:-:|
| Circle | 2 |
| Mana | 6 |
| Chance Not to Be Disrupted (%) | (Evaluating Intelligence + Meditation + Inscription) / 4, up to `75` |
| Casting Delay (s) | +0.5 |
| Minimum Duration (s) | 15 |
| Maximum Duration (s) | Magery * 2 |
