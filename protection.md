# Protection
Protection adds to the caster's armor rating without blocking Meditation, unlike worn armor. It can't be recast while active.

| Property | Value |
|:-|:-:|
| Circle | 2 |
| Mana | 6 |
| Armor | (Evaluating Intelligence + Meditation + Inscription) / 4 |
| Minimum Duration (s) | 15 |
| Maximum Duration (s) | Magery * 2 |
