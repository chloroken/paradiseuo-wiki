## Connectors
These routes exist to connect Britannia and the Lost Lands. Recall, Mark, and Gate Travel don't work in the Lost Lands, so these are the only ways in or out. Fire and Ice also connect the two, but they're true dungeons, listed under [T2A Dungeons](#t2a-dungeons).

### Passages
| Passage | Britannia Side | Lost Lands Side |
|:-|:-:|:-:|
| [Britain Sewers](/britain_sewers) | Ladders in the center of Britain | Swamp east of Papua |
| [Delucia Passage](/delucia_passage) | Mountains southwest of Trinsic | Delucia |
| [North Mines](/north_mines) | Mountains north of Minoc | Southeast of Papua |
| Marble Island Passage | A building on Marble Island, south of Britain | West of Terathan Keep |
| Vesper Cemetery Passage | Stairs in a building in Vesper's cemetery | Cemetery south of Papua |

### Other Routes
| Route | Britannia Side | Lost Lands Side | How It Works |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Mage shop teleporters | Mage shop in north Moonglow | Mage shop in Papua | Saying `recdu` in Moonglow or `recsu` in Papua |
| Serpent pillars | The sea east of Trinsic and west of Jhelom | The sea west of the desert | Saying `doracron` or `sueacron` from a boat |
| Yew Prison | Teleport altar in Yew Prison | Lighthouse at the north end of the desert | Stepping onto the altar |
| Temple Isle | Temple on the island south of Trinsic | Temple east of Delucia | Walking into the stone table |

The serpent pillars only work from a boat within `10` tiles of a pillar. See [Commands: Places](/commands#places).

### Graveyards and Ruins
Graveyards and ruins are scattered across Britannia. They're smaller than dungeons but have monsters of their own, and graveyards hold level 1 dungeon chests. See [Lockpicking: Dungeon Chests](/lockpicking#dungeon-chests).

### Camps
Camps of orcs, ratmen, lizardmen, and brigands appear in the wilds. Each camp has a few guards around a campfire and two chests: an unlocked level 1 chest and a locked level 3 chest.

Each camp also holds a prisoner in chains, either a noble or a seeker of adventure, who calls for help when a player comes near. Freeing the prisoner starts an escort. See [Quests](/world#quests).

A camp lasts until both chests are emptied or the prisoner is freed or killed, then disappears `5` minutes later.
