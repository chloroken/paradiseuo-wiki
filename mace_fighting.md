# Mace Fighting
Mace Fighting is the skill of fighting with blunt weapons: maces, hammers, staves, and the shepherd's crook. One-handed maces weaken a monster's blows, two-handed maces and staves slow its attacks, and the crook bonks. [Blacksmithing](/blacksmithing), [Carpentry](/carpentry), and [Herding](/herding) each strengthen one group.

## Weapons
| Type | Hands | Weapons | Effect | Scaling Skill |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| One-handed maces | One | Club, hammer pick, mace, maul, war mace | Sapping vigor | |
| Two-handed maces | Two | War axe, war hammer | Crushing Blow | Blacksmithing |
| Staves | Two | Black staff, gnarled staff, quarter staff | Crushing Blow | Carpentry |
| Shepherd's crook | Two | Shepherd's crook | Shillelagh | Herding |

- One-handed maces do modest damage, but leave a hand free for a shield.
- Two-handed maces hit a little harder, and can splash onto every monster around the target.
- The quarter staff does about as much damage as a sword at `100` Carpentry.
- The shepherd's crook is weak on its own, but bonks for up to `10×` damage with Herding.

The war axe is a two-handed mace that uses Mace Fighting. It can still chop trees, but gets no damage bonus from [Lumberjacking](/lumberjacking).

Each weapon's damage and speed are listed on [Damage Per Second](/dps). Tactics, Strength, and Anatomy raise damage the same way as for any other weapon. See [Combat: Damage](/combat#damage). Mace hits don't drain the target's stamina, but blunt weapons wear down armor faster. See [Armor: Durability](/armor#durability).

## Defense
While holding any of these weapons, a character defends with Mace Fighting. See [Hit Chance](/combat#hit-chance). Only one-handed maces leave a hand free for a shield. See [Parrying](/parrying).

## Sapping Vigor
Each hit with a one-handed mace saps a monster's vigor, so its melee attacks hit as if its Strength were lower, down to half. The amount is the damage dealt, scaled by [Arms Lore](/arms_lore). The sap wears off `30` seconds after the last one-handed mace hit. See [Weapon Effects: Sapping Vigor](/weapon_effects#sapping-vigor).

## Crushing Blow
Two-handed maces and staves land a Crushing Blow on `Arms Lore / 400` of hits, `25%` at `100` Arms Lore. It makes a monster attack at half speed for `10` seconds. See [Weapon Effects: Crushing Blow](/weapon_effects#crushing-blow).

With a two-handed mace, each Crushing Blow has a `Blacksmithing / 100` chance to splash, hitting every monster within `1` tile of the target. Staves never splash.

Only players land weapon effects. Hits with every Mace Fighting weapon except the shepherd's crook can train Arms Lore.

## Staves
Carpentry adds up to `15%` damage to black, gnarled, and quarter staves, for players only. Staves take both hands, so they can't be used with a wooden shield. See [Weapons: Scaling Skills](/weapons#scaling-skills).

## Shillelagh
The first shepherd's crook hit after a `7` second cooldown bonks for up to `10×` the crook's damage roll, depending on Herding, [Magery](/magery), and the attacker's mana. A bonk never misses, and a hit with any other weapon restarts the cooldown. The crook never lands a Crushing Blow and doesn't need Arms Lore. See [Weapon Effects: Shillelagh](/weapon_effects#shillelagh).
