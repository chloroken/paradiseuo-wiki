# Mace Fighting
Mace Fighting is the skill of fighting with blunt weapons: maces, hammers, staves, and the shepherd's crook. While holding any of them, a character defends against an attacker's [hit chance](/combat#hit-chance) with Mace Fighting, but only one-handed maces leave a hand free for [parrying](/parrying) with a shield. Each weapon has its own [speed and damage](/dps), which Tactics, Strength, and [Anatomy](/anatomy) raise the same way as for any other weapon. Mace hits don't drain the target's stamina, but blunt weapons [wear down armor](/armor#durability) faster.

| Type | Hands | Weapons | Effect | Scaling Skill |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| One-handed maces | One | Club, hammer pick, mace, maul, war mace | [Sapping vigor](/weapon_effects#sapping-vigor) | |
| Two-handed maces | Two | War axe, war hammer | [Crushing Blow](/weapon_effects#crushing-blow) | [Blacksmithing](/blacksmithing) |
| Staves | Two | Black staff, gnarled staff, quarter staff | Crushing Blow | [Carpentry](/weapons#scaling-skills) |
| Shepherd's crook | Two | Shepherd's crook | [Shillelagh](/weapon_effects#shillelagh) | [Herding](/herding) |

- One-handed maces sap a monster's vigor, weakening its blows, and leave a hand free for a shield.
- Two-handed maces and staves slow a monster's attacks with Crushing Blow, powered by [Arms Lore](/arms_lore). With Blacksmithing, a two-handed mace's Crushing Blow also splashes onto every monster around the target.
- The quarter staff does about as much damage as a sword at `100` Carpentry.
- The shepherd's crook is weak on its own, but bonks hard with Herding and [Magery](/magery), and never lands a Crushing Blow.

The war axe is a two-handed mace that uses Mace Fighting. It can still chop trees, but gets no damage bonus from [Lumberjacking](/lumberjacking).
