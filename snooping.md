# Snooping
Snooping is the thief's eye for valuables. It works mostly in the background of [Stealing](/stealing), finding the best item in a pocket, and it also adds to the chance of [begging](/begging#begging-for-mercy) a monster for mercy.

## Spotting Prizes
Every theft from a monster or townsperson rolls Snooping, succeeding `Snooping%` of the time. On a success, if the pocket holds a [lottery prize](/stealing#lottery) or a town prize, the thief sees *Your keen eye finds something valuable!* and steals the prize. Otherwise they grab a random item from the pocket.

Without Snooping, a prize is buried among the gold and gems, so reaching it can take several thefts, each with its own chance of getting caught. With townsfolk, the digging can be skipped by [peeking into their backpack](#peeking-into-backpacks) first.

## Peeking into Backpacks
A backpack shown on a townsperson's paperdoll can be double-clicked from within `1` tile to peek inside. It opens on a success, which is `Snooping%` of the time. A peek also fills the townsperson's pocket the same way a theft does, and items in an open pocket can be stolen one by one.

- Only members of the [Thieves' Guild](/guilds#thieves-guild) can peek into townsfolk's backpacks, even in guarded towns. In the [Karma towns](/stealing#karma-towns), a peek also needs the town's Karma.
- Monsters have no paperdolls, so their pockets are only reached by stealing.
- A pocket holding a prize shows *Something valuable is tucked away in here.* when it opens.
- Every peek costs `4` Karma.
- A failed peek reveals a hidden snooper, unless they pass a `(Hiding / 2)%` chance to stay hidden.

## Manual Training
Snooping trains as a thief steals, since every theft from a monster or townsperson rolls it. A thief who works the pockets of the world will find it rising on its own.
