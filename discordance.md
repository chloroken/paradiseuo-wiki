# Discordance
Discordance uses a musical instrument to weaken an enemy.

## Effect
Discordance lowers the target's [stats](/stats) and [skills](/skills) for a short, random duration, weakening both its defense and offense. The reduction is `(Discordance / 5)%`, capping at `20%` at grandmaster.

| Discordance | Reduction (%) |
|:-|:-:|
| 10 | 2 |
| 50 | 10 |
| 100 | 20 |

## Duration
Discordance lasts `5` to `12.5` seconds. When it would expire, there is a `33%` chance to extend it by another `5` to `12.5` seconds, with no limit on extensions. The expected total duration, including extensions, is `13.125` seconds.

If the bard becomes hidden, through a skill like [Hiding](/hiding) or a spell like [Invisibility](/invisibility), Discordance ends in `15` seconds and is not extended again.

## Cooldown
Discordance has a fixed `5` second cooldown, whether it succeeds or fails.

## Demibosses
[Demibosses](/demibosses) can be discorded whatever their bard difficulty. After a good performance with [Musicianship](/musicianship), the chance to succeed is `Discordance / 200`, so `50%` at grandmaster, and a success weakens them like any other target. Attempts on a demiboss never raise Discordance. Peacemaking and Provocation still have no effect on demibosses.
