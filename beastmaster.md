# Beastmaster
The Beastmaster leads a pack of powerful pets and uses bardic music to control enemies. Peacemaking and Provocation allow it to manage encounters by calming monsters or turning them against each other. Drums, while not required, are highly thematic.

The Beastmaster is one of the strongest hunting builds because of Animal Taming and Provocation, however it's the only build that doesn't take Magery that also doesn't take any of the Valor skills. Thus, combat relies on calculated barding and taming play with little room for error, and travel relies on moongates, boats, walking, or friends, which can complicate a follower setup. With 100 Dexterity, bandages reach the 3-second breakpoint and carrying capacity is maximized.
