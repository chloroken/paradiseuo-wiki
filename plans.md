# Plans
*Paradise* is in alpha, and thus receives constant development. This page serves as a way of keeping track of the bigger picture: getting the game suitable for beta.

## Fixes
- Bard discordance should work on bosses to give them a solid use in bossing
- No shovel vendor?

## Classes
- Thief (noncombat, uses stealing/snooping in dungeons)
  - Begging
  - Snooping
  - Stealing
  - Hiding
  - Stealth
  - Lockpicking
  - Remove Trap
- NPCs can call guards which send you to jail, increasingly as chain caught

## Skills
Skills should have adventure potential, or some aspect that makes them not feel "useless" to take on a main character suitable for dungeon diving.

These skills have an obvious way to add adventure potential:
- Alchemy (more healing from potions)
- Cooking (food buff, or eating heals you?)
- Tailoring (cloth gives small absorb based on inscription)
- Tinkering (spider drone toy?)

These skills create an alternate playstyle:
- Begging (pacify a hostile target, 100% at lv100 snoop+steal)
- Snooping (creates new lootpacks in monster bags, deleted on death)
- Stealing (allows stealing from monsters)

These skills don't have an obvious way to add adventure potential:
- Camping
- Detecting Hidden
- Fishing
- Forensic Evaluation
- Item Identification
- Remove Trap

These skills had adventure potential added or enhanced:
- Blacksmithing (more 1h mace dmg)
- Carpentry (more staff dmg, more wood shield armor)
- Fletching (Up to +2 range, half the delay before shooting after moving, more ammunition recovered)
- Herding (shillelagh bonk mechanic)
- Hiding (Ambush, for 1.5× damage)
- Mining (makes pickaxe dmg competitive)
- Poisoning (Poison chance on hit tied to level)
- Spirit Speak (Longer summons, resistance to Dispel, stronger elementals that never fail to cast at 100)
- Stealth (Ambush up to 2.5× damage, with extra accuracy)
- Tracking (Ranged accuracy; at 100, ranged shots never miss fleeing targets)

These skills already had adventure potential:
- Anatomy (Weapon damage, Valor regeneration, weapon special chance, unarmed defense, and better bandages)
- Animal Lore (Pets obey more reliably; counts toward stable slots)
- Animal Taming (Pets that fight for the tamer)
- Archery (Bows and crossbows, and defense while holding one)
- Arms Lore (Weapone effect trigger chance)
- Cartography (treasure mapper
- Discordance (Lowers a target's stats and skills by up to 20%)
- Evaluating Intelligence (Spell damage; unarmed defense with Anatomy)
- Fencing (Increases accuracy and enables weapon specials)
- Healing (Bandages that heal, cure poison, and resurrect)
- Inscription (Stronger Reactive Armor and Protection)
- Lockpicking (picks locks in dungeons)
- Lumberjacking (Up to +30% axe damage)
- Mace Fighting (Increases accuracy and enables weapon specials)
- Magery (All spells, but it weakens Valor)
- Meditation (Mana regeneration; stronger Reactive Armor and Protection)
- Musicianship (Success for every bard skill)
- Parrying (Shield blocks; Valor toughness, up to 20% less damage taken)
- Peacemaking (Calms targets)
- Provocation (Turns monsters against each other)
- Resisting Spells (Resists hostile spells)
- Swordsmanship (Increases accuracy and enables weapon specials)
- Tactics (Weapon damage from -50% to +50%; Valor accuracy up to +30)
- Veterinary (Pet healing with up to 5 tiles of range; stable slots)
- Wrestling (Unarmed fighting, Disarm and Stun, defense for mages)

## Dungeons
- All dungeons get a page with lore
- All dungeon teleporters are checked
- All get a spawner overhaul
- All get a demiboss
- All floors should have "something neat", either a tougher monster, some site, some unique traps, some lore, something that makes the floor not feel like it's an afterthought. for example in shame, floor 1 just has earth ele and scorpions, but adding skeletons to the "old campsites" for the miners made the floor interesting.

## Wiki
- Have page written for every skill
- Use template to make page for every spell
- Add pages with incoming links that are missing

## Projects
- Fill world with trainers, like shepherds in farms
- Rework stealing/snooping into something more. Stealing from npcs/monsters should be a whole thing.
- Karma Town Trinkets to complete reputation rework
- Crafting overhaul & expansion, allow for solo economy with NPCs (npcs in towns sometimes offer jobs to crafters. weighted based on town, e.g., yew more likely to offer fletchers a job.), give guilds a purpose
- Give gems some type of meaningful purpose outside of economy, maybe as a secondary currency?
- More quests from npcs in towns, including hauling quests, boating quests, etc.
- Fork TazUO and customize defaults for new players, strip egregious branding
- Tinker with UO client and add lines to Cliloc.ENU to enable "profession" selection in client
- Create "dungeon dive" videos just for fun, highly stylized, appealing for beta testers (when alpha is done)
