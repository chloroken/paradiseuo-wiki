# Connectors
Connectors are the passages and routes between Britannia and the Lost Lands. Recall, Mark, and Gate Travel don't work in the Lost Lands, so these routes and the Fire and Ice dungeons are the only ways in or out. Fire and Ice are true dungeons, so they're listed under [T2A Dungeons](/dungeons#t2a-dungeons).

## Passages
These caves and tunnels lead from Britannia to the Lost Lands on foot.

| Passage | Britannia Side | Lost Lands Side |
|:-|:-:|:-:|
| [Britain Sewers](/britain_sewers) | Ladders in the center of Britain | Swamp east of Papua |
| [Delucia Passage](/delucia_passage) | Mountains southwest of Trinsic | Delucia |
| [North Mines](/north_mines) | Mountains north of Minoc | Southeast of Papua |
| Marble Island Passage | A building on Marble Island, south of Britain | West of Terathan Keep |
| Vesper Cemetery Passage | Stairs in a building in Vesper's cemetery | Cemetery south of Papua |

## Other Routes
| Route | Britannia Side | Lost Lands Side | How It Works |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Mage shop teleporters | Mage shop in north Moonglow | Mage shop in Papua | Saying `recdu` in Moonglow or `recsu` in Papua |
| Serpent pillars | The sea east of Trinsic and west of Jhelom | The sea west of the desert | Saying `doracron` or `sueacron` from a boat |
| Yew Prison | Teleport altar in Yew Prison | Lighthouse at the north end of the desert | Stepping onto the altar |
| Temple Isle | Temple on the island south of Trinsic | Temple east of Delucia | Walking into the stone table |

The serpent pillars only work from a boat within `10` tiles of a pillar, by saying their [words](/commands#places).
