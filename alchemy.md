# Alchemy
Alchemy is the skill of brewing potions from reagents. Alchemists heal, cure, strengthen, and poison, and their explosion potions are one of the few ways to damage a whole group without Magery.

## Brewing
Double-click a mortar and pestle to open the crafting menu. Every potion needs reagents and an empty bottle. A failed attempt loses the reagents but returns the bottle.

The chance to succeed rises evenly from `0%` at a potion's minimum skill to `100%` at its maximum. Some potions have a minimum below `0`, so even a character with no Alchemy can sometimes brew them. See [Crafting](/crafting).

Potions of the same kind stack.

| Potion | Minimum Skill | Maximum Skill | Reagents |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Refresh | -25 | 25 | 1 black pearl |
| Lesser heal | -25 | 25 | 1 ginseng |
| Night sight | -25 | 25 | 1 spider's silk |
| Lesser cure | -10 | 40 | 1 garlic |
| Lesser poison | -5 | 45 | 1 nightshade |
| Lesser explosion | 5 | 55 | 3 sulfurous ash |
| Heal | 15 | 65 | 3 ginseng |
| Agility | 15 | 65 | 1 bloodmoss |
| Poison | 15 | 65 | 2 nightshade |
| Total refresh | 25 | 75 | 5 black pearl |
| Cure | 25 | 75 | 3 garlic |
| Strength | 25 | 75 | 2 mandrake root |
| Greater agility | 35 | 85 | 3 bloodmoss |
| Explosion | 35 | 85 | 5 sulfurous ash |
| Greater strength | 45 | 95 | 5 mandrake root |
| Greater heal | 55 | 105 | 7 ginseng |
| Greater poison | 55 | 105 | 4 nightshade |
| Greater cure | 65 | 115 | 6 garlic |
| Greater explosion | 65 | 115 | 10 sulfurous ash |
| Deadly poison | 90 | 140 | 8 nightshade |

Even a grandmaster alchemist only brews deadly poison `20%` of the time.

## Potions
### Healing
Heal potions restore hit points. After drinking one, another heal potion can't be drunk for `10` seconds.

| Potion | Hit Points Healed |
|:-|:-:|
| Lesser heal | 3 – 10 |
| Heal | 6 – 20 |
| Greater heal | 9 – 30 |

### Curing
Cure potions have a chance to cure poison, depending on the potion and the poison's strength. See [Poison: Potions](/poison#potions).

### Refresh
Refresh potions restore stamina: `25%` of maximum stamina for a refresh potion, and all of it for a total refresh potion.

### Strength and Agility
Strength potions raise Strength, and agility potions raise Dexterity, for `2` minutes. Regular potions add `10`, and greater potions add `20`.

### Night Sight
A night sight potion lights up the drinker's surroundings, like the [Night Sight](/night_sight) spell.

### Poison
Poison potions are used with [Poisoning](/poisoning) to coat weapons, arrows, bolts, and food. See [Poison](/poison).

### Explosion
Explosion potions are thrown at a target. They explode after a short fuse, damaging everyone within `2` tiles. A thrown potion adds `Alchemy / 10` damage, and each explosion does at most `40` damage. A potion that explodes in the thrower's backpack also damages the thrower.

| Potion | Damage |
|:-|:-:|
| Lesser explosion | 5 – 10 |
| Explosion | 10 – 20 |
| Greater explosion | 15 – 30 |
