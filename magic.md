# Magic
Magic covers casting spells with Magery, defending against them, and the bard skills that fight with music instead of steel.

## Spellcasting
- [Magery](/magery): the skill of casting spells, for damage, healing, utility, and travel.
- [Spells](/spells): every spell by circle, with the words spoken to cast it.
- [Resisting Spells](/resisting_spells): a chance to shrug off part of a hostile spell.

## Protective Spells
- [Protection](/protection): keeps a caster's spells from being disrupted.
- [Reactive Armor](/reactive_armor): absorbs melee damage and reflects part of it back.
- [Magic Reflection](/magic_reflection): bounces the next offensive spell back at its caster.

## Bard Skills
- [Peacemaking](/peacemaking): calms a target so it stops attacking.
- [Provocation](/provocation): makes two targets fight each other.
- [Discordance](/discordance): weakens an enemy.
