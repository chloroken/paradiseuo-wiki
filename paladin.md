# Paladin
The Paladin is a warrior who combines physical combat with defensive magic. Mace Fighting drains enemy stamina with every hit, slowing their attacks. Limited Magery preserves most of the Valor bonuses while providing access to essential healing, curing, and buff spells. Brings some Dexterity to speed up bandage use on others, and for faster mace swings.

## Skills
- 100 Mace Fighting
- 100 Anatomy
- 100 Healing
- 100 Parrying
- 100 Tactics
- 100 Resisting Spells
- 40 Magery
- 60 Meditation
