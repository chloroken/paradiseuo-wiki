# Lockpicking
Lockpickers use [lockpicks](/lockpick) to pick locks. These locks often secure containers full of loot, which the lockpicker can then take. A [skeleton key](/skeleton_key) can be used instead of a lockpick for a better chance of success.

## Town Chests
Town chests are scattered through buildings in towns. A chest's difficulty is tied to the NPCs who live in the building, and its loot usually matches the type of building. A chest in an alchemist's shop might contain potions, for example.

| Chest Level | Minimum Skill | Maximum Skill | Buildings |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Level 1 | 0 | 30 | Alchemist, Baker, Bard, Butcher, Carpenter, Clothier, Cobbler, Docks, Farm, Healer, Herbalist, Inn, Library, Merchant, Mill, Mine, Observatory, Painter, Stables, Tavern, Thieves' Guild, Tinker, Veterinarian |
| Level 2 | 30 | 60 | Armorer, Blacksmith, Bowyer, Jeweler, Mage, Ranger, Tanner, Weaponsmith, Provisioner |
| Level 3 | 60 | 90 | Fighters Guild, Guards |

## Dungeon Chests
Dungeon chests are found in dungeons and hold traditional dungeon loot. They range from level 1 (graveyards and orc camps), to levels 2 and 3 (dungeons), to level 4 (deep, difficult dungeons).

| Chest Level | Minimum Skill | Maximum Skill |
|:-|:-:|:-:|
| Level 1 | 43 | 52 |
| Level 2 | 68 | 72 |
| Level 3 | 76 | 84 |
| Level 4 | 82 | 92 |

## Treasure Chests
[Treasure maps](/treasure_map) spawn a treasure chest of level 1 to 5.

| Chest Level | Minimum Skill | Maximum Skill | Gain Limit |

| Level 1 | 0 | 10 | 50 |
| Level 2 | 40 | 50 | 90 |
| Level 3 | 60 | 70 | 100 |
| Level 4 | 75 | 85 | 100 |
| Level 5 | 90 | 100 | 100 |
