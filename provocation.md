# Provocation
Bards use a musical instrument to make two targets fight each other.

The chance to provoke two monsters rises with [Musicianship](/musicianship), and stronger monsters are more likely to ignore it.

Provocation has a `10` second cooldown on success and a `5` second cooldown on failure.

After `60` seconds, provocation ends if a provoked target loses sight of the bard or its target. Fleeing monsters walk away instead of fighting, even when provoked.

## Manual Training
Monsters in the Moonglow Zoo can't be provoked onto each other, only onto the bard herself.
