# Forgotten One
The Forgotten One is a demiboss locked in a room on floor 3 of [Deceit](/deceit). No one remembers what he was before the dead of Deceit claimed him, and he has never forgiven them for it. Unlike the other demibosses, he fights only in melee, and he is built for a pair of steady tanks rather than a crowd. After he dies, he returns `12` to `24` hours later.

## Reaching the Forgotten One
His room's door is locked. The only way in is the pit in the entrance room of floor 4, which drops into his room on floor 3. Anyone who leaves the room has to fight back down through the dungeon to return.

He can also appear anywhere in Deceit. Every use of a summoning brazier has a `0.1%` chance to call him instead of an ordinary monster. A summoned Forgotten One drops the same loot, but like any summon, he vanishes `1` hour after he appears, even in the middle of a fight. See [Deceit: Summoning Braziers](/deceit#summoning-braziers).

## Fighting the Forgotten One
- He has `15,000` to `20,000` hit points.
- He is extremely fast, and as a demiboss he walks around obstacles to reach his target, so he can't be kited.
- He attacks constantly, and his hits carry Regular [poison](/poison). His damage is low, but the poison comes back faster than it can be cured, so healing through it doesn't work.
- He never switches targets on his own, so whoever holds him keeps him until they pass him to someone else.
- If he leaves his room, he blinks back into it.
- Poison and bleeding have no effect on him.
- As a demiboss, his hits do double damage to [followers](/follower#followers-in-combat).

## Loot
| Drop | Amount | Chance (%) |
|:-|:-:|:-:|
| Gold | 5,260 average | 100 |
| Weapons, armor, shields, and jewelry | 9 average | 100 |
| Potions | 500 | 100 |
| Level 5 [treasure map](/treasure_map) | 1 | 100 |
| Spiteful memory | 1 | 10 |

Most of the weapons, armor, and shields are magic, but jewelry never is. The potions come in `5` stacks of `100`, each a random choice of greater agility, explosion, greater strength, greater heal, greater cure, greater explosion, or deadly poison. Stacks of the same kind merge.

## Spiteful Memory
Spiteful memory is a pair of magical gold earrings, all that remains of the Forgotten One's grudge against the liches of Deceit.

While spiteful memory is worn, a weapon hit on a lich or lich lord, melee or ranged, drains all of its mana. The wearer sees *Your earrings drain the lich's mana.*, and the lich shows *\*mana drained\**. The earrings can drain a lich once every `5` minutes. A hit on a lich with no mana doesn't use up the wait. The Lich King is unaffected.

**Spiteful memory crumbles if a lich or lich lord kills its owner.** Every spiteful memory the player carries, worn or in their backpack, is destroyed, and they see *Forgotten again...* Death to the Lich King or anything else leaves it intact.
