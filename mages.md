# Mages
Mages always take [Magery](/magery) and Meditation. Mages who expect to be caught in melee defend with Wrestling or a weapon, while those who stay out of reach can take [Tailoring](/tailoring) and wear a [robe](/meditation#robes) for faster mana regeneration.

| Class | Unique Qualities |
|:-|:-:|
| Battlemage | Combines polearm hits and spells, and halves a caster's Intelligence |
| Golemancer | Builds its own golems and keeps one fighting almost nonstop |
| Monk | Stuns casters out of their spells with its fists |
| Priest | Heals a whole group with spells and bandages, and resurrects two ways |
| Shepherd | Bonks for up to ten times damage, then follows up with a spell |
| Summoner | Fields all four elementals at once |
| Wizard | Casts from a robe, fires wands between spells, and refills its mana with sweets |

## Battlemage
The Battlemage is a classic T2A halberd mage. It combines the full spellbook with the slow, crushing hits of a polearm, landing a big swing and following it with a damage spell for burst damage few others can match. Its polearm's [Concussion Blows](/weapon_effects#concussion-blow), powered by [Anatomy](/anatomy), halve a target's Intelligence, which devastates other casters.

Casting a spell unequips the polearm, but a polearm swings so slowly that there's plenty of time to cast between swings and pick it back up. Its full Magery gives it every utility and travel spell, and [Evaluating Intelligence](/evaluating_intelligence) makes its damage spells hit hard.

With `100` Magery, it gets none of [Valor](/valor)'s bonuses. It defends with [Swordsmanship](/swordsmanship) only while the polearm is in its hands, so it's defenseless every time it casts. It wears leather to keep its mana regeneration up, which leaves its body exposed.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `25` Dexterity, `100` Intelligence
- Skills: Swordsmanship, Tactics, Anatomy, Magery, Evaluating Intelligence, Meditation, and Resisting Spells
- Gear: polearm and spellbook, with leather
- Defense: Swordsmanship while holding its polearm and resisted spells
- Healing: spells, unbonused potions
- Weakness: no defense while casting with its polearm put away

## Golemancer
The Golemancer is a full mage who builds its own fighting machines. [Tinkering](/tinkering) builds its [golems](/golem), and [Inscription](/inscription) brings them to life, so it can make and use the strongest terror golems. Its Magery lets it scribe its own Blade Spirits scrolls for them, so its main cost is gems.

It keeps a golem fighting almost nonstop, unleashing a new one as soon as the last winds down, and adds damage spells powered by Evaluating Intelligence on top. Monsters ignore golems, so they come for the Golemancer instead, and it has to hold them off itself. Inscription raises its [Reactive Armor](/reactive_armor) and [Protection](/protection) to their strongest for exactly that, and its robe keeps its mana flowing.

Every golem costs gems, and now and then one breaks down for good. With no Wrestling or weapon skill, it has no real melee defense beyond its spells, so it survives by keeping its protections up rather than by avoiding hits.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `25` Dexterity, `100` Intelligence
- Skills: Magery, Meditation, Evaluating Intelligence, Tailoring, Tinkering, Inscription, and Resisting Spells
- Gear: golems and spellbook, with a robe
- Defense: resisted spells and strengthened Reactive Armor and Protection
- Healing: spells, unbonused potions
- Weakness: every golem costs gems, and monsters always come for the Golemancer

## Monk
The Monk fights with fists and magic, a martial artist with a full spellbook. It wears leather so its mana regeneration isn't slowed, and keeps its hands free for both punching and casting. Wrestling defends it whether it's punching or casting, so it's never caught without a defense.

Its signature is the [stun](/wrestling#wrestling-moves). Every punch that lands can try to freeze the target for `4` seconds, once every `10` seconds, succeeding half the time at grandmaster. A stunned caster can't start a spell, so the Monk shuts down enemy mages better than anyone, and frozen monsters give it time to cast. Fighting in melee means being hit while casting, so it takes Inscription, which with Meditation and Evaluating Intelligence gives it the strongest Reactive Armor and Protection. Hits are absorbed and rarely interrupt its spells.

Fists do little damage, especially without Tactics, so the Monk does most of its damage with spells, and it gets none of Valor's bonuses with `100` Magery. Leather is its only armor, so it relies on its spells, Wrestling, and stuns to stay safe.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `25` Dexterity, `100` Intelligence
- Skills: Magery, Evaluating Intelligence, Meditation, Wrestling, Anatomy, Inscription, and Resisting Spells
- Gear: fists and spellbook, with leather
- Defense: Wrestling, stuns, the strongest Reactive Armor and Protection, and resisted spells
- Healing: spells, unbonused potions
- Weakness: fists do little damage, and leather is its only armor

## Priest
The Priest is a group healer. It mends allies with Magery and bandages at the same time and cures poison with both. It also has two ways to resurrect: the Resurrection spell and [bandage resurrection](/healing#resurrection), which its Healing and Anatomy make possible, so when one fails it can try the other. A group with a Priest can push much deeper than one without.

It stays close to the group, healing whoever is hurt. Its robe regenerates mana fastest when it's low, and [Cooking](/cooking#eating-as-a-cook) lets it eat sweets to refill mana in the middle of a fight, so it rarely runs dry. It counts on the group to keep monsters off it.

Without Evaluating Intelligence, its damage spells are weak, so it does little damage on its own and is slow to solo. It has no Wrestling or weapon skill either, so anything that reaches it hits almost every time. It's built to keep others alive, not to kill.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `40` Dexterity, `85` Intelligence
- Skills: Magery, Meditation, Healing, Anatomy, Cooking, Tailoring, and Resisting Spells
- Gear: spellbook, bandages, and sweets, with a robe
- Defense: Reactive Armor, resisted spells, and its group
- Healing: spells, food, bandages, unbonused potions
- Weakness: no Evaluating Intelligence or Wrestling, so it does little damage and can't hold off what reaches it

## Summoner
The Summoner is a conjurer whose [summons](/summon) do the fighting. [Spirit Speak](/spirit_speak) makes its summons last up to twice as long, resist being dispelled, and take fewer follower slots, and it makes its elementals hit harder and its summon spells never fail at grandmaster. With enough Spirit Speak, it can field all four elementals at once.

It stays back while its summons hold the line. Its Wrestling defends it while it casts, so whatever slips past its summons hits it far less often. It wears a robe, which regenerates mana fastest when its mana is low, and eats sweets to refill mana after a round of summoning.

Its own damage spells are weak without Evaluating Intelligence, so it depends on its summons for damage. It has no weapon and wears only a robe, and enemies that dispel its summons, or [demibosses](/demibosses) that do double damage to them, take away most of its strength.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `25` Dexterity, `100` Intelligence
- Skills: Magery, Meditation, Tailoring, Cooking, Wrestling, Resisting Spells, and Spirit Speak
- Gear: spellbook, with a robe
- Defense: Wrestling, resisted spells, and its summons
- Healing: spells, food, unbonused potions
- Weakness: its own damage spells are weak, and it loses most of its strength when its summons are dispelled

## Wizard
The Wizard is a pure damage caster that never stops casting. Where other mages run dry and meditate, it keeps its mana up three ways: a robe that regenerates mana fastest when it's nearly empty, sweets that refill it in the middle of a fight, and [wands](/magic_items#wands) that cast without any mana at all.

It fights from range, casting damage spells powered by Evaluating Intelligence and firing a wand whenever it needs one more spell. [Alchemy](/alchemy) more than doubles what its heal potions do, so it can heal without spending mana that should go to damage. In a long fight, it outlasts other casters.

It has no way to avoid melee hits. No Wrestling, no weapon skill, and no armor mean anything that reaches it hits almost every time, and only its Reactive Armor soaks up the first few blows. It has to keep its distance and kill quickly. Wands are found as loot and run out of charges, so it can't count on them forever.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `25` Dexterity, `100` Intelligence
- Skills: Magery, Meditation, Evaluating Intelligence, Tailoring, Alchemy, Cooking, and Resisting Spells
- Gear: spellbook and wands, with a robe
- Defense: range, Reactive Armor, and resisted spells
- Healing: bonused potions, spells, food
- Weakness: no way to avoid melee hits
