# Stable
Animal trainers offer stables to keep pets safe while their owners are offline. They respond to these commands:

| Command | Effect |
|:-|:-:|
| `stable` | Stables a pet for 30 gold, taken from the backpack or bank |
| `claim` | Opens a list of stabled pets to claim |
| `claim <name>` | Quick-claims a pet by name |
| `stablecount` | Shows how many stable slots are used and available |

- Stabled pets can be claimed from any animal trainer.
- Pack animals must be unloaded before they can be stabled.
- Dead, summoned, and human creatures can't be stabled.

## Stable Slots
Stable slots depend on a character's combined Animal Taming, Animal Lore, and Veterinary skill:

| Combined Skill | Stable Slots |
|:-|:-:|
| Below 160 | 2 |
| 160 – 199.9 | 3 |
| 200 – 239.9 | 4 |
| 240+ | 5 |

Each point above `95` in Animal Taming, Animal Lore, or Veterinary adds `1` more slot, so a character at `100` in all three has `20` slots.
