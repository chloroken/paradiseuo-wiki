# Fighter
The Fighter is a traditional warrior who relies on physical combat and defensive skills. With no Magery, the Fighter receives the full benefits of Valor, making it exceptionally durable. It can use any type of weapon, and will typically have at least two sets for different situations.

## Skills
- 100 Swordsmanship, Mace Fighting, or Fencing
- 100 Archery or a second weapon skill
- 100 Tactics
- 100 Anatomy
- 100 Parrying
- 100 Resisting Spells
- 100 Healing
