# Anatomy
Anatomy is the knowledge of how bodies work. It makes every weapon hit harder, lets wrestlers [stun](/wrestling#wrestling-moves), and lets polearms land [Concussion Blows](/weapon_effects#concussion-blow) without Arms Lore. It strengthens [bandages](/healing#healing-wounds), and is needed to cure poison and resurrect with them. Alongside Evaluating Intelligence, it lets an empty-handed character [defend without Wrestling](/wrestling#unarmed-defense). Through [Valor](/valor), it also makes hit points regenerate faster for characters who fight without magic.

## Examining
Using the skill and targeting a person or creature describes how strong and agile it is. From `65` Anatomy, it also describes how much stamina the target has left. Items can't be examined.

## Damage
Anatomy adds `1%` weapon [damage](/combat#damage) for every `5` points, plus `10%` at grandmaster, for `30%` at `100`. It applies to every weapon, including bows and bare hands.

| Anatomy | Damage Bonus (%) |
|:-|:-:|
| 25 | 5 |
| 50 | 10 |
| 75 | 15 |
| 100 | 30 |
