# World
*Paradise* uses the Felucca version of the Britannia map, including the Lost Lands. This page covers the people, quests, and points of interest found across it. Monsters and dungeons have pages of their own: see [Monsters](/monsters) and [Dungeons](/dungeons).

## Towns
See: [Towns](/towns)

## Dungeons
See: [Dungeons](/dungeons)

## NPCs
Non-player characters, or NPCs, offer services and trade, and some can even help in combat.

- [Stablemasters](/stable) keep pets safe while their owners are offline.
- Escortables ask to be led to another town, as part of a quest.
- Blacksmiths repair weapons and armor.

Two towns only serve characters of the right Karma: Serpent's Hold for the good, and Buccaneer's Den for the evil. 𐠒 See [Reputation](/reputation#karma-towns).

## Quests
*Paradise* has the following quests:

- Escort NPCs: double-click the NPC.
- Seekers of Adventure: double-click the NPC.
- Prison Camps (escort or seeker): say `destination`, then say `i will take thee`.
- Witch's Apprentice: double-click Grizelda.

## Points of Interest
- Cyclops Valley: Home of titans and nightmares
- The Oasis: Home of the silver steed
- Shrines: There are eight of them scattered across Britannia
- Moonglow Zoo: Creatures of all variety with a `24` hour respawn
- Tortoise Lagoon: Home of frenzied ostards
