# World
*Paradise* uses the Felucca version of the Britannia map, including the Lost Lands. This page covers the people, quests, and points of interest found across it, and links to the places, dangers, and property found there.

## Exploring
- [Towns](/towns): banks, shops, trainers, stables, and moongates.
- [Dungeons](/dungeons): where the strongest monsters and best loot are found.
- [Monsters](/monsters): what roams the wilds and fills the dungeons.
- [Treasure Map](/treasure_map): maps to buried chests, decoded with Cartography and opened with Lockpicking.
- [House](/house): placing and keeping a home.
- [Boat](/boat): traveling by sea and river.

## NPCs
Non-player characters, or NPCs, offer services and trade, and some can even help in combat.

- [Animal trainers](/stable) keep pets safe while their owners are offline.
- Escortables ask to be led to another town, as part of a quest.
- Blacksmiths repair weapons and armor.

Two towns only serve characters of the right Karma: Serpent's Hold for the good, and Buccaneer's Den for the evil. See [Karma Towns](/reputation#karma-towns).

## Quests
*Paradise* has the following quests:

- Escort NPCs: started by double-clicking the NPC
- Seekers of Adventure: started by double-clicking the NPC
- Prison Camps (escort or seeker): started by saying `destination`, then `i will take thee`
- Witch's Apprentice: started by double-clicking Grizelda

## Points of Interest
- Shrines: eight of them, scattered across Britannia
- Moonglow Zoo: creatures of all kinds, with a `24`-hour respawn
