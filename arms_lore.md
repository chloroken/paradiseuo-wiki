# Arms Lore
Arms Lore is the knowledge of weapons and armor. It controls the [weapon effects](/weapon_effects) that make each weapon type distinct, and it can judge the condition and quality of a weapon or piece of armor.

## Weapon Effects
Each of these weapon effects scales with Arms Lore, from nothing at `0` to full strength at `100`. A character without Arms Lore can use any weapon, but never lands its effect. Only players land weapon effects.

| Effect | Weapons | At 100 Arms Lore |
|:-|:-:|:-:|
| Critical strikes | Swords | Each sword's own chance |
| Sapping vigor | One-handed maces | Vigor equal to the damage dealt |
| Crushing Blow | Two-handed maces, staves except the shepherd's crook | 25% of hits |
| Paralyzing Blow | Pitchforks, short spears, spears | 25% of hits |

At `50` Arms Lore, each chance is halved, and one-handed maces sap half as much vigor. A two-handed mace's [Blacksmithing](/blacksmithing) splash only happens on a Crushing Blow, so it needs Arms Lore too.

### Effects Without Arms Lore
Some effects don't use Arms Lore at all:
- The shepherd's crook's [shillelagh](/weapon_effects#shillelagh) scales with [Herding](/herding) and [Magery](/magery).
- Polearms' [Concussion Blow](/weapon_effects#concussion-blow) uses [Anatomy](/anatomy), on `25%` of hits at `100`.
- Wrestling's [stun](/weapon_effects#stun) needs Wrestling and Anatomy.
- [Crossbow volleys](/archery#crossbow-volley), [poison](/poisoning), and [ambushes](/ambush) aren't weapon effects.

A fighter who doesn't want weapon effects can skip Arms Lore and spend those skill points elsewhere. See [Character: Classes](/character#classes).

## Training on Hits
Every hit with a weapon that has an Arms Lore effect has a chance to raise Arms Lore: swords, maces, staves, and two-handed spears. Weapons without an Arms Lore effect don't train it: two-handed axes, polearms, krysses, daggers, war forks, knives, the pickaxe, the shepherd's crook, bows, crossbows, and bare hands.

## Examining
Using the skill and targeting a weapon or piece of armor within `2` tiles describes its condition. For a weapon, it also gives a rough idea of its damage and says if it has poison on it. For armor, it gives a rough idea of its protection, and for armor of [colored metal](/armor#metal-types), how well it wards off magic: *faintly* for dull copper and shadow iron, *some* for copper and bronze, *well* for gold and agapite, and *exceptionally well* for verite and valorite. Items for sale on a player vendor can be examined too. A failed attempt gives no information. Each attempt, failed or not, has a chance to raise Arms Lore.
