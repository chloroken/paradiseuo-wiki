# Stats
There are three stats: Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence.

Players have up to `225` stat points to distribute as they wish. However, `100` Strength is highly recommended for almost every [class](/classes), to have enough hit points to survive.

Stats rise when related [skills](/skills) are trained, but only when those skills could be increased. Training a skill while it's maxed or not set to increase will not result in stat gain.

## Strength
Strength increases hit points, damage, and carrying capacity.

To gain Strength quickly, train skills like Camping, Herding, and Mining.

## Dexterity
Dexterity increases stamina and attack speed.

To gain Dexterity quickly, train skills like Snooping, Musicianship, and Camping.

## Intelligence
Intelligence increases mana and mana regeneration.

To gain Intelligence quickly, train skills like Herding, Camping, and Spirit Speak.
