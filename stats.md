# Stats
There are three stats: `strength`, `dexterity`, and `intelligence`.

A player may have up to 225 stat points distributed as they wish. However, 100 strength is highly recommended for almost all [classes](/Classes) to have sufficient health to survive enemies.

These stats are leveled up when related [skills](/Skills) are trained, but only when those skills *could be increased*. Training a skill while it's maxed or not set to increase will not result in stat gain.

## Strength
Strength increases `hitpoints`, `damage`, and `carry capacity`.

To quickly gain strength, train skills like Camping, Herding, and Mining.

## Dexterity
Dexterity increases `stamina` and `attack speed`.

To quickly gain dexterity, train skills like Snooping, Musicianship, and Camping.

## Intelligence
Intelligence increases `mana` and `mana regeneration`.

To quickly gain intelligence, train skills like Herding, Camping, and Spirit Speak.
