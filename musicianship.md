# Musicianship
Musicianship is the skill of playing musical instruments. Every bard skill needs a good performance first, so Musicianship decides how often a bard's music works at all. The bard skills are [Peacemaking](/peacemaking), [Provocation](/provocation), and [Discordance](/discordance).

## Playing
Double-clicking an instrument within `1` tile plays it, well or badly. The chance to play well is the character's Musicianship as a percentage: `50%` at `50`, and always at `100`.

## Bard Skills
Each attempt at a bard skill rolls Musicianship first, with the same chance. A failed roll ends the attempt with *You play poorly, and there is no effect.*, and the bard skill itself isn't rolled.

| Musicianship | Attempts Lost to a Poor Performance (%) |
|:-|:-:|
| 50 | 50 |
| 80 | 20 |
| 90 | 10 |
| 100 | 0 |

A bard skill plays the last instrument the bard played or chose, as long as it's still in their backpack. Otherwise, the bard is asked to choose one.

## Instruments
Drums, tambourines, lap harps, lutes, and standing harps all play the same way, and differ only in sound. Instruments are made with [Carpentry](/carpentry), which needs some Musicianship. See [Carpentry: Instruments](/carpentry#instruments).

- Bard skills wear out instruments. An instrument has `350` to `450` uses, and breaks with *The instrument played its last tune.* when the last one is used.
- An exceptional instrument has twice the uses, and makes every target `5` points easier for bard skills, about `10%` more chance to succeed.
- Instruments found as loot are always plain, with no slayer or other properties.
