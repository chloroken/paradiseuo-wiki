# Shard Features
*Paradise* uses The Second Age (T2A) era as a baseline for everything on this page. This page lists only the major ways *Paradise* differs from T2A. Smaller tweaks are listed in the [patch notes](/patch_notes).

## Archer Rework
Stealth archers can ambush their targets. Breaking Hiding with a ranged weapon attack does `1.5×` damage, and every stealthed step before the shot adds more damage and accuracy. After `10` steps, the shot can't miss and does `2.0×` damage. A target can only be ambushed once per minute. See [Archery](/archery).

## Tamer Rework
Animal tamers can bandage pets from further away, up to `5` tiles at grandmaster [Veterinary](/veterinary), instead of only from an adjacent tile. They can also attack their own pets without becoming criminals, which makes pets usable for training combat skills and Veterinary. During alpha, Animal Taming gains skill twice as fast as normal. Pets always keep pace with their owner, even when the owner is mounted. Silver steeds and nightmares have a `1%` chance to spawn as Shiny, with a rare hue. See [Mount](/mount).

## Wrestler Rework
Wrestling moves happen automatically, with no button or macro needed. When an empty-handed punch hits and the moves are off cooldown, armed players and human NPCs are disarmed, and everything else is stunned. The moves cost no stamina, and each attempt starts a `10` second cooldown. See [Weapon](/weapon).

## Carrying Capacity
In T2A, only Strength affects how much a character can carry. On *Paradise*, Dexterity adds half as much as Strength: `1.75` stones per point, compared to `3.5`. A character with `100` Strength and `100` Dexterity can carry `565` stones instead of `390`. See [Stats](/stats).

## PvP
The world uses Trammel rules on the Felucca map, so players can't attack each other anywhere. Duel arenas and guild PvP are the only ways to fight other players. Players also can't steal from other players, though items dropped on the ground can still be taken.

## Reputation
Fame gives a choice between better gem prices at high Fame and faster skill gain at low Fame, while Karma decides who can trade in Serpent's Hold and Buccaneer's Den. Templars and thugs outside those towns make Karma easy to raise or lower. See [Reputation](/reputation).

## T2A Monsters
Only monsters from T2A and earlier roam the world, with the silver steed as the one exception. Creatures added by later expansions, such as plague beasts, boglings, juka, and greater dragons, have been removed, and the dungeons added after T2A are closed.

## Travel
Mark, Recall, and Gate Travel don't work in dungeons or the Lost Lands. Buccaneer's Den has no moongate, and Serpent's Hold can also be reached by a teleporter north of Trinsic.

## Decay
Houses decay after `90` days without a refresh, and everything inside is destroyed rather than dropped on the ground. Boats sink `24` hours after their last refresh, to stop players blocking objectives and islands with boats.
