# Features
*Paradise* uses The Second Age (T2A) era as its baseline. This page lists the ways *Paradise* deliberately differs from T2A: reworks and other novel changes. Changes that bring the shard closer to T2A, and smaller tweaks, are listed in the [patch notes](/patch_notes).

## Fighter Rework
Fighters are reworked to keep pace with mages, tamers, and bards in PvM. Each part rewards a different skill or style:
- Antimage: characters with little or no Magery get three combat bonuses, each from its own skill. Tactics improves accuracy, Parrying reduces the physical damage taken, and Anatomy speeds up hit point regeneration. Each bonus grows with its skill and shrinks as Magery rises, and only players get them. See [Antimage](/combat#antimage).
- Ambush: stealth archers can ambush their targets. Breaking Hiding with a ranged weapon attack does bonus damage, and every stealthed step before the shot adds more damage and accuracy, until the shot can't miss. Monsters and animals can't be ambushed again right away. See [Archery](/archery).
- Poisoning: fencing weapons, arrows, and bolts can be coated with poison, to give Poisoning the respect in combat that it deserves. Anyone can use them, but without Poisoning, every swing or shot risks poisoning the user, so poisoners get the most from them. See [Poisoning](/poisoning).
- Wrestling: moves happen automatically when an empty-handed punch hits, with no button or macro needed. See [Wrestling Moves](/wrestling#wrestling-moves).
- Carrying capacity: Dexterity adds carrying capacity, half as much per point as Strength, so fighters who raise Dexterity can carry more of their gear. See [Carrying Capacity](/stats#carrying-capacity).

## Reputation
Fame gives a choice between better gem prices at high Fame and faster skill gain at low Fame, while Karma decides who can trade in Serpent's Hold and Buccaneer's Den. Templars and thugs outside those towns make Karma easy to raise or lower, and locked Karma is unlocked with shrine mantras. See [Reputation](/reputation).

## Other Changes
- The world uses Trammel rules on the Felucca map, so players can't attack or steal from each other anywhere. Duel arenas and guild wars are the only PvP, and items dropped on the ground can still be taken.
- Animal tamers can bandage pets from further away as their Veterinary skill rises. See [Veterinary: Range](/veterinary#range).
- Players can attack their own pets without becoming criminals, and their own pets appear green to them. See [Training on Pets](/pet#training-on-pets).
- Pets keep pace with their owner, even when the owner is mounted.
- During alpha, Animal Taming gains skill twice as fast as normal.
- Silver steeds and nightmares have a small chance to spawn Shiny, with a rare hue. See [Mount](/mount).
- Houses decay if they aren't refreshed, and everything inside is destroyed rather than dropped on the ground. See [House](/house).
- Boats sink soon after their last refresh, to stop players blocking objectives and islands with boats. See [Boat](/boat).
- Saying `claim` to an animal trainer opens a list of stabled pets to choose from. See [Stable](/stable).
- Ore can be smelted straight from a pack animal's backpack. See [Crafting](/crafting).
