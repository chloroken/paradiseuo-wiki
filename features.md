# Features
*Paradise* uses The Second Age (T2A) era as its baseline. This page lists the significant ways that *Paradise* deliberately differs from T2A.

Smaller tweaks are listed in the [patch notes](/patch_notes).

## Fighter Rework
Fighters are enhanced to keep pace with mages, tamers, and bards in PvM.

- [Valor](/combat#valor):
  - Characters who fight without magic are gripped by [the warrior's virtue](https://wiki.ultimacodex.com/wiki/Valor).
  - Valor grants fighting bonuses that diminish as Magery increases.
- [Armor](/armor#how-armor-works):
  - Armor reduces damage by a percentage, so it protects against hard hits as well as light ones.
  - Shields also reduce every hit, on top of blocking.
- [Wrestling Moves](/wrestling#wrestling-moves):
  - Wrestling moves happen automatically, no button or macro needed.
  - Automatically chooses a move based on skill levels and target.
- [Poisoning](/poisoning):
  - Only fencing weapons can be poisoned.
  - Inflicting poison on hit is now tied to Poisoning level.
- [Critical Strikes](/weapon#other-effects-on-hit):
  - Swords can critically strike for double damage, more often with higher Swordsmanship.
- [Carrying Capacity](/stats#carrying-capacity):
  - Dexterity adds carrying capacity, half as much per point as Strength.

## Archer Rework
Archers receive several enhancements that make ranged combat more effective.

- Valor and Poison:
	- Archers also benefit from Valor bonuses.
	- Arrows and bolts can be coated with poison.
- [Fletching](/fletching#fletching-and-archery):
  - Increases range, reduces time to shoot, and saves more arrows.
- [Ambush](/archery#ambush):
	- Shots do bonus damage if they break Hiding.
  - Breaking Stealth further enhances the shot.
- [Tracking](/tracking#archer-accuracy):
	- Increases accuracy with ranged weapons.
  - Shots against fleeing targets never miss.

## Taming Enhancements
Animal tamers get several changes that make training, healing, and keeping pets easier:

- During alpha, Animal Taming gains skill twice as fast as normal.
- Players can attack their own pets without becoming criminals, and their own pets appear green to them. See [Training on Pets](/pet#training-on-pets).
- Animal tamers can bandage pets from further away as their Veterinary skill rises. See [Veterinary: Range](/veterinary#range).
- Pets keep pace with their owner, even when the owner is mounted.
- Saying `claim` to an animal trainer opens a list of stabled pets to choose from. See [Stable](/stable).
- Silver steeds and nightmares have a small chance to spawn Shiny, with a rare hue. See [Mount](/mount).

## Reputation
Fame gives a choice between better gem prices at high Fame and faster skill gain at low Fame, while Karma decides who can trade in Serpent's Hold and Buccaneer's Den. Templars and thugs outside those towns make Karma easy to raise or lower, and locked Karma is unlocked with shrine mantras. See [Reputation](/reputation).

## Other Changes
- The world uses Trammel rules on the Felucca map, so players can't attack or steal from each other anywhere. Duel arenas and guild wars are the only PvP, and items dropped on the ground can still be taken.
- Houses decay if they aren't refreshed, and everything inside is destroyed rather than dropped on the ground. See [House](/house).
- Boats sink soon after their last refresh, to stop players blocking objectives and islands with boats. See [Boat](/boat).
- Ore can be smelted straight from a pack animal's backpack. See [Crafting](/crafting).
