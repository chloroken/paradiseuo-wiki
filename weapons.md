# Weapons
Each type of weapon is trained with its own skill, and most types have a [weapon effect](/weapon_effects) of their own, mostly powered by [Arms Lore](/arms_lore). Every weapon follows the same rules for [hit chance](/combat#hit-chance), [swing speed](/combat#swing-speed), and [damage](/combat#damage), and their numbers are compared on [Damage Per Second](/dps). Fencing weapons, knives, arrows, and bolts can be [poisoned](/poisoning). Looted weapons can be [magic](/magic_weapons) and crafted ones exceptional, but never both.

## Scaling Skills
Some gathering and crafting skills make their own weapons hit harder, for players only. Each bonus grows evenly to two thirds of its total at `99.9`, with the last third at grandmaster, and it adds to the Strength and Anatomy bonuses to damage.

| Scaling Skill | Weapons | Bonus at 50 (%) | Bonus at 100 (%) |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Lumberjacking | Axes, except the pickaxe | 10 | 30 |
| Carpentry | Black, gnarled, and quarter staves | 5 | 15 |
| Mining | Pickaxe | 33.3 | 100 |

Hits with axes, except the pickaxe, can train Lumberjacking, but hits never train Carpentry or Mining. Two more skills scale weapons in other ways: Herding powers the shepherd's crook's [shillelagh](/weapon_effects#shillelagh), Blacksmithing lets a two-handed mace's [Crushing Blow](/weapon_effects#crushing-blow) splash onto every monster around the target, and Carpentry lets a stave's Crushing Blow flourish onto every monster around the wielder. Carpentry also makes the wooden shield the sturdiest [shield](/parrying#shields) against weapons.
