# Magic Untrap
Magic Untrap disarms a chest's trap from a distance, but only on level `1` [dungeon chests](/lockpicking#dungeon-chests) and level `1` [treasure chests](/treasure_map). On any other trapped chest, including town chests, the spell fizzles with *This trap is beyond your magic.* Every stronger trap needs [Remove Trap](/remove_trap), or a willingness to take the hit. The chest doesn't need to be unlocked first.

| Property | Value |
|:-|:-:|
| Circle | 2 |
| Mana | 6 |
| Words | An Jux |
| Reagents | Bloodmoss and sulfurous ash |
| Range (tiles) | 12 |
