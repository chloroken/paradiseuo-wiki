# Forager
The Forager is a dedicated gatherer who specializes in collecting raw materials from the world. Fishing, Mining, and Lumberjacking provide access to valuable resources, while Magery allows the Forager to Recall home with a full load.

100 Strength and 100 Dexterity maximize carrying capacity. The remaining skill slot can be used for crafting or another skill that complements gathering, such as Hiding, which keeps the Forager safe in the field.

## Skills
- 50+ Magery
- 100 Mining
- 100 Lumberjacking
- 100 Fishing
- 100 Arms Lore
- 100 Item Identification
- 100 Hiding or any other skill
