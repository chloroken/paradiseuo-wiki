# Magery
Magery is the skill of casting spells. It offers damage, healing, utility, and the best ways to travel, which is why most characters take it. Every point of Magery also weakens [Valor](/combat#valor), the warrior's virtue that strengthens fighters. A fighter has to choose between spells and Valor.

## Spells
Magery has `64` spells, split evenly across `8` circles. Each spell needs reagents and mana, and is cast from a spellbook or a scroll. See [Spells](/spells).

## Casting Chance
Each circle has a range of Magery where its spells can fail. Below the range a spell always fails, above it the spell always succeeds, and in between the chance rises evenly.

| Circle | Mana | Can Cast From | 50% Chance At | Never Fails At |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 1st | 4 | 0 | 0 | 20 |
| 2nd | 6 | 0 | 14.3 | 34.3 |
| 3rd | 9 | 8.6 | 28.6 | 48.6 |
| 4th | 11 | 22.9 | 42.9 | 62.9 |
| 5th | 14 | 37.1 | 57.1 | 77.1 |
| 6th | 20 | 51.4 | 71.4 | 91.4 |
| 7th | 40 | 65.7 | 85.7 | 105.7 |
| 8th | 50 | 80 | 100 | 120 |

At `100` Magery, 7th circle spells succeed `86%` of the time and 8th circle spells `50%` of the time.

Casting from a scroll counts as two circles lower, so a scroll of an 8th circle spell is cast with the chances of a 6th circle spell. A grandmaster never fails a scroll of any circle.

## Related Skills
- [Evaluating Intelligence](/evaluating_intelligence) raises spell damage, and gives unarmed defense alongside Anatomy.
- [Meditation](/meditation) regenerates mana faster.
- [Inscription](/inscription) makes scrolls, and strengthens [Reactive Armor](/reactive_armor) and [Protection](/protection).
- [Resisting Spells](/resisting_spells) protects against spells, and a caster's Magery makes their spells harder to resist.

## Travel
Recall, Mark, and Gate Travel are the fastest ways to cross the world, but none of them work in dungeons or the Lost Lands.

## Manual Training
Every cast, successful or not, has a chance to raise Magery, as long as the caster's skill is inside that circle's range in the casting chance table. Once a circle never fails, it stops training Magery, so the circle to practice rises with skill.
