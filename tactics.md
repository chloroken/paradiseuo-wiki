# Tactics
Tactics is the skill of fighting effectively. It raises damage with every weapon, and through [Valor](/valor) it also raises accuracy for fighters with little Magery.

## Damage
Tactics changes weapon damage by `(Tactics - 50)%`. It applies to every weapon, including bows and bare hands.

| Tactics | Damage Bonus (%) |
|:-|:-:|
| 0 | -50 |
| 25 | -25 |
| 50 | 0 |
| 75 | +25 |
| 100 | +50 |

Below `50`, Tactics lowers damage, so a character who fights with no Tactics does half damage. Tactics is applied first, and the bonuses from Strength, [Anatomy](/anatomy), and [scaling skills](/weapons#scaling-skills) multiply the result, so Tactics makes every other [damage bonus](/combat#damage) worth more.

## Manual Training
Every hit with any weapon, including bare hands, has a chance to raise Tactics. Attacking one's own [follower](/follower) isn't criminal, so a tamed animal makes a safe training partner.

## Macro Training
Tactics has no active use, so it can't be macroed on its own. It only rises through fighting.
