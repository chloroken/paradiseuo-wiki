# Tactics
Tactics is the skill of fighting effectively. It raises damage with every weapon, and for fighters without Magery it also raises accuracy.

## Damage
Tactics changes weapon damage by `(Tactics - 50)%`. It applies to every weapon, including bows and bare hands.

| Tactics | Damage Bonus (%) |
|:-|:-:|
| 0 | -50 |
| 25 | -25 |
| 50 | 0 |
| 75 | +25 |
| 100 | +50 |

Below `50`, Tactics lowers damage, so a character who fights with no Tactics does half damage. Tactics is applied first, and the bonuses from Strength, Anatomy, and Lumberjacking multiply the result, so Tactics makes every other damage bonus worth more. See [Combat: Damage](/combat#damage).

## Accuracy
Through Antimage, Tactics also improves accuracy for characters with less than `100` Magery. The bonus grows with Tactics and shrinks as Magery rises. See [Antimage](/combat#antimage).

## Manual Training
Every hit with any weapon, including bare hands, has a chance to raise Tactics. Attacking one's own [pet](/pet) isn't criminal, so a tamed animal makes a safe training partner.

## Macro Training
Tactics has no active use, so it can't be macroed on its own. It only rises through fighting.
