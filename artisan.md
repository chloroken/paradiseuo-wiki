# Artisan
The Artisan is a dedicated crafter who produces equipment and supplies for others. It combines several crafting skills to cover a wide range of production needs instead of focusing on a single industry.

Magery is required for Inscription, and also provides Recall for convenient travel, but doesn't have to be maxed out. An Artisan who skips both Magery and Inscription can use 100 Dexterity instead for additional carrying capacity.

## Skill Plans
With Inscription:
- 80+ Magery
- 100 Inscription
- 100 Meditation
- 4 other crafting skills

Without Inscription:
- 50+ Magery
- 6 crafting skills

Without Magery:
- 7 crafting skills

## Crafting Skills
- 100 Alchemy
- 100 Blacksmithing
- 100 Carpentry
- 100 Cooking
- 100 Fletching
- 100 Inscription
- 100 Tailoring
- 100 Tinkering
