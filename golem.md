# Golem
Golems are small fighting machines built with [Tinkering](/tinkering) and brought to life with [Inscription](/inscription). A golem is carried as a device and unleashed in the field, where it attacks nearby monsters for `30` seconds and then winds down. Golems are [followers](/follower), but they aren't [summons](/summon).

## Golems
There are three golems. Each needs the same amount of Tinkering and Inscription to craft and to unleash.

| Golem | Tinkering and Inscription | Follower Slots | Damage | Weight (stones) | Hue |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Prototype golem | 50 | 1 | 3 – 5 | 10 | Copper |
| Mechanical golem | 75 | 2 | 6 – 10 | 15 | Shadow iron |
| Terror golem | 100 | 3 | 10 – 14 | 20 | Valorite |

Golems attack about as fast as a blade spirit, so they do roughly `5`, `10`, and `15` damage per second.

## Crafting
Golems are crafted with a tinker's tools, under `Golems`. Crafting needs both skills, and it can raise both. Every golem takes `1` Blade Spirits scroll and `3` gems.

| Golem | Ingots | Parts | Gems |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Prototype golem | 5 | 2 gears, 2 springs | Amber, citrine, and tourmaline |
| Mechanical golem | 10 | 4 gears, 4 springs, 2 clock parts | Ruby, amethyst, and sapphire |
| Terror golem | 15 | 6 gears, 6 springs, 4 clock parts | Emerald, star sapphire, and diamond |

The chance to succeed is `50%` at the required Tinkering and rises to `100%` at `50` points above it. A grandmaster tinker always succeeds with a prototype golem, succeeds `75%` of the time with a mechanical golem, and succeeds `50%` of the time with a terror golem. Golems are made from the [Tinkering menu](/tinkering#golems).

## Unleashing
Double-clicking a golem in the backpack unleashes it beside its owner. It can't be unleashed:
- Without enough Tinkering and Inscription
- Without enough free follower slots
- Within `30` seconds of unleashing another golem of any kind

Unleashing a golem doesn't raise any skill or reveal a hidden owner, and golems can be unleashed in town.

## Fighting
A golem can't be commanded. It attacks the closest red or gray creature, and walks around obstacles to reach it. It never attacks players, blue creatures, NPCs, or anyone's followers. Gray creatures in town are fair game, and attacking them isn't a crime, so guards ignore it.

Golems are invulnerable, and monsters ignore them. A golem can't hold a monster's attention, so its owner still has to survive the fight. Its owner gets loot rights to anything it damages, the same as with any other follower.

Golems aren't summons. [Spirit Speak](/spirit_speak) doesn't make them last longer or hit harder, and Dispel and Mass Dispel don't affect them.

## Winding Down
A golem winds down after `30` seconds. It winds down early if its owner dies, logs out, or moves more than `18` tiles away, and every golem winds down when the server restarts.

When a golem winds down, there's a `90%` chance it drops back onto the ground where it stood, as a golem that can be picked up and used again. Its owner sees *Your golem winds down.* Otherwise, the owner sees *Your golem breaks down beyond repair.*
