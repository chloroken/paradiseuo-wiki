# Inscription
Inscription is the skill of writing [spell scrolls](/scroll) and runebooks with a scribe's pen. A scribe can only write a spell that's in a [spellbook](/spellbook) they carry, and needs enough [Magery](/magery) to have a chance of casting it. Each scroll takes a blank scroll, one of each of the spell's reagents, and mana. A failed attempt ruins the scroll and loses the materials. Inscription also strengthens [Reactive Armor](/reactive_armor) and [Protection](/protection), and brings [golems](/golem) to life alongside Tinkering.

## Runebooks
A runebook holds up to `16` marked runes and casts Recall or Gate Travel from any of them using its own charges. Writing one takes `45` to `95` Inscription, `8` blank scrolls, a Recall scroll, a Gate Travel scroll, and an unmarked recall rune.

A runebook's maximum charges are `5`, plus `1` for normal quality or `2` for exceptional, plus `1` for every `30` Inscription, up to `10`. A grandmaster writes runebooks with `9` charges, or `10` when they come out exceptional.

## Manual Training
Inscription trains by writing whatever scrolls succeed about half the time. A scribe who also casts can use what they write, so little goes to waste.
