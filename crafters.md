# Crafters
Crafters make and gather what everyone else uses, and defend themselves with what they carry.

| Class | Unique Qualities |
|:-|:-:|
| Artisan | Crafts nearly everything: gear, potions, scrolls, and golems |
| Forager | Gathers ore, wood, and fish, and cooks its catch in the field |

## Artisan
The Artisan is the town's workshop in one character. It [crafts](/crafting) metal weapons and armor, wooden furniture and staves, clothing and leather armor, potions, scrolls, and tools, and Recalls between workshops to keep production moving. Nearly everything other classes use can come from an Artisan.

It isn't helpless outside town. With [Tinkering](/tinkering) and [Inscription](/inscription), it can build and unleash [golems](/golem) to defend itself, and its [Magery](/magery) gives it healing and utility spells. Its [Alchemy](/alchemy) more than doubles what its own heal potions do, and its [Tailoring](/tailoring) lets it wear a robe for faster mana regeneration.

It has no weapon skill and no defensive skills, so it avoids real fights. Its value is in what it makes for others, not what it can kill.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `25` Dexterity, `100` Intelligence
- Skills: Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Carpentry, Inscription, Magery, Tailoring, and Tinkering
- Gear: crafting tools and golems, with a robe
- Defense: golems and spells
- Healing: spells and stronger heal potions
- Weakness: no weapon skill and no defensive skills

## Forager
The Forager gathers raw materials for everyone else. It mines ore, chops wood, and catches fish, then marks its own runes and Recalls home with the load. Its stats balance carrying capacity against mana, so it can haul a heavy load and still Recall back and forth.

It defends itself with the axe or pickaxe it already carries, which [Lumberjacking](/lumberjacking) and [Mining](/mining) make hit hard, using [Swordsmanship](/swordsmanship) and [Tactics](/tactics). When a fight goes badly, it cooks the fish it catches and eats them for burst healing, and its Magery adds healing spells.

It's a weak fighter, with no [Anatomy](/anatomy), no [Parrying](/parrying), and `100` Magery that leaves it no [Valor](/valor). It can't hide from what it can't beat, so it has to pick its gathering spots carefully.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `65` Dexterity, `60` Intelligence
- Skills: Mining, Lumberjacking, Fishing, Swordsmanship, Tactics, Magery, and Cooking
- Gear: axe or pickaxe, with leather
- Defense: Swordsmanship with its axe or pickaxe
- Healing: spells and food
- Weakness: a weak fighter that can't hide from what it can't beat

## Prospector
The Prospector is a miner who fights back. [Mining](/mining) doubles the damage of the pickaxe at grandmaster, turning a gathering tool into a weapon that keeps up with any sword. It fights with the same pickaxe it digs with, so it can head into a dungeon, clear a path, and mine the ore at the end of it without changing gear.

With a pickaxe in one hand and a shield in the other, it gets full Valor: accuracy, toughness, and regeneration. It's a sturdy, simple fighter that blocks, hits hard, and bandages itself, curing poison with its Anatomy. Mining also makes digging up [treasure](/treasure_map) easier, and its ore can go to a blacksmith for [colored metal](/armor#metal-types) armor.

The pickaxe has no weapon effect, so the Prospector has no Crushing Blows, critical strikes, or other tricks. It's straightforward damage and nothing else, and like every fighter it travels on foot.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `100` Dexterity, `25` Intelligence
- Skills: Mining, Swordsmanship, Tactics, Anatomy, Parrying, Healing, and Resisting Spells
- Gear: pickaxe and shield, with plate
- Defense: Swordsmanship, shield blocks, full Valor, and resisted spells
- Healing: bandages
- Weakness: the pickaxe has no weapon effect, so it has little utility
