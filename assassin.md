# Assassin
The Assassin specializes in deadly precision, combining Fencing with Poisoning to stifle and eliminate enemies before they know what hit them. Fast thrusting weapons allow the Assassin to apply poison pressure while Anatomy and Tactics improve melee damage. Without Healing, the Assassin requires perfect positioning and timing, or Healing can be taken at the cost of having to eat more spells.

It's worth noting that spellcasters will generally prioritize curing poison before casting offensive spells, so by repetitively applying poisons, the Assassin can effectively mitigate incoming magic damage.
