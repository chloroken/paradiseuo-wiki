# Deceit
Deceit is a [dungeon](/dungeons) on Dagger Isle with four floors. The restless dead fill its frozen halls, from the skeletons and zombies of floor 1 to the liches and lich lords of floor 4. Ancient summoning braziers can call any of its monsters from the dark, and something forgotten waits behind a locked door on floor 3.

## History
Deceit lies on Dagger Isle in the frozen north of Britannia, and it is bound to the virtue of [Honesty](https://wiki.ultimacodex.com/wiki/Honesty). The Avatar once braved its depths for the [Blue Stone of Honesty](https://wiki.ultimacodex.com/wiki/Virtue_Stones). Since then the dead have claimed it. Liches tend the braziers that their masters once used to call servants from the grave. The braziers still burn for anyone who lights them, and what they summon serves no one.

## Floor 1
Skeletons and zombies shamble through floor 1. On the lower level, a skeleton prison holds a mass of skeletons, overseen by a bone knight. Water elementals rise from the pools, and a wraith guards the stairs to floor 2.

## Floor 2
Skeletons, zombies, ghouls, shades, and wraiths roam floor 2. A water elemental lurks in the pool. Bone and skeletal magi gather in a ritual chamber. A magical teleporter to floor 3 is guarded by a lich and bone and skeletal knights and magi.

## Floor 3
Skeletons, ghouls, shades, spectres, and wraiths fill floor 3. Liches performing rituals and experiments can be found all over. Fountains make water elementals, and poison elementals emanate from toxic pools. The furthest chamber holds the stairs to floor 4.

A locked room on this floor holds the [Forgotten One](/forgotten_one), a [demiboss](/demibosses) with `15,000` to `20,000` hit points who returns `12` to `24` hours after he dies.

## Floor 4
Skeletons crowd every corner of floor 4, and there's a bone pit brimming with them. Fire elementals burn in a hall with lava pits.

The deepest chamber is the lich lord room, where liches and lich lords gather, seemingly experimenting on a silver serpent.

The pit in floor 4's entrance room drops into the Forgotten One's room on floor 3. It is the only way in, so anyone who leaves the room has to fight back down through the dungeon to return to the fight.

## Curiosities

### Summoning Braziers
Seven ancient summoning braziers burn in Deceit: one in the main hall of floor 1, one in the ritual chamber of floor 2, two in the furthest chamber of floor 3, and three in the lich lord room on floor 4.

Double-clicking a brazier from within `2` tiles lights it with *\*The brazier burns with dark energy\**. After `2` seconds, a monster appears beside it, *\*summoned from the brazier\**. If there is no room nearby, the summoning fails. The brazier then lies dormant for `5` minutes, even if the summoning failed.

Each summoning picks one of these monsters:

- skeleton, zombie, ghoul, shade, spectre, and wraith
- bone knight, skeletal knight, bone mage, and skeletal mage
- water elemental, poison elemental, and fire elemental
- lich, lich lord, and silver serpent

Thus, each choice has an equal `6.25%` chance of being selected. After a choice is selected, there is a `0.1%` chance to call the Forgotten One instead, regardless of floor.

Monsters summoned from braziers belong to no one. They attack anyone nearby, wander the dungeon like any other monster, and drop their normal loot. Each one vanishes `1` hour after it was summoned, even in the middle of a fight. If others use the braziers, adventurers would be wise to expect stronger monsters being present throughout the dungeon.
