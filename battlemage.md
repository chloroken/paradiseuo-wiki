# Battlemage
The Battlemage combines offensive magic with powerful two-handed weapon attacks.

It casts a spell, then follows up with a halberd that can land a [Concussion Blow](/weapons#concussion-blow), halving the target's Intelligence and lowering its maximum mana. Anatomy also adds `30%` to the halberd's damage. When casting spells without the halberd equipped, Anatomy and Evaluating Intelligence let the Battlemage defend as if it had `110` Wrestling. See [Wrestling: Unarmed Defense](/wrestling#unarmed-defense).

Full Magery means the Battlemage gets no [Valor](/combat#valor) bonuses. Even so, it's one of the strongest dueling builds. It can line up extreme burst damage, such as an Explosion followed by a halberd swing and an Energy Bolt, while concussing its opponent.
