# Battlemage
The Battlemage combines offensive magic with powerful two-handed weapon attacks. It casts a spell before following up with a halberd, using Wrestling for defense while its hands are free to cast.

Full Magery means the Battlemage receives no Valor bonuses. Without Anatomy, the halberd also cannot land a Concussion Blow. Despite these limitations, it remains one of the strongest dueling builds due to its ability to line up extreme burst damage with combinations like Explosion + halberd swing + Energy Bolt.
