# Monsters
Monsters roam the wilds and fill the [dungeons](/dungeons). They range from rats and slimes near town to dragons and daemons in the deepest dungeons, and many of them can be tamed or turned against each other.

## T2A Monsters 𐠒
Only monsters from T2A and earlier roam the world, with the silver steed as the one exception. Creatures added by later expansions, such as plague beasts, boglings, juka, and greater dragons, have been removed, and the dungeons added after T2A are closed. See [Shard Features](/features#t2a-monsters).

## Behavior 𐠒
Monsters on *Paradise* behave differently from standard ServUO:
- They notice players from a shorter distance.
- They don't path around obstacles, so a wall or a tree can block their approach.
- Monsters that flee walk away instead of running.
- Monsters with Magery cast more often the higher their Magery, and less often the lower it is.
- Monsters only cast Teleport when they're stuck, and never cast Invisibility.

## Fighting Monsters
Monsters hit harder in melee than in standard ServUO, which got T2A's monster damage wrong. Characters with little Magery take less physical damage through [Antimage](/combat#antimage). See [Combat](/combat).

Outside of dungeons, monsters won't attack [Young](/young_player) players unless the Young player attacks first.

## Dealing With Monsters
- Many animals and monsters can be tamed. See [Animal Taming](/animal_taming) and [Pet](/pet).
- Bards can turn monsters against each other with [Provocation](/provocation), or calm them with [Peacemaking](/peacemaking).

## Loot
Monsters carry gold and items, and stronger monsters can drop [treasure maps](/treasure_map). Some loot has been removed on *Paradise*:
- Necromancer scrolls and reagents
- Seeds
- Fire horns
