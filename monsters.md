# Monsters
Monsters roam the wilds and fill the [dungeons](/dungeons). They range from rats and slimes near town to dragons and daemons in the deepest dungeons, and many of them can be tamed or turned against each other.

## T2A Monsters
Only monsters from T2A and earlier roam the world, with two exceptions: the silver steed, and [demibosses](#demibosses) unique to *Paradise*. Creatures added by later expansions, such as plague beasts, boglings, juka, and greater dragons, have been removed, and the dungeons added after T2A are closed.

## Behavior
Monsters behave differently from later versions of Ultima Online:
- They notice players from a shorter distance.
- They don't path around obstacles, so a wall or a tree can block their approach.
- Monsters that flee walk away instead of running.
- Monsters with Magery cast more often the higher their Magery, and less often the lower it is.
- Monsters only cast Teleport when they're stuck, and never cast Invisibility.

## Fighting Monsters
Monsters hit players with their full melee damage, twice as hard as on most ServUO shards. Their melee hits do `25%` more damage to [pets](/pet), and pets do `25%` less damage to them. Characters with Parrying and little Magery take less physical damage through [Valor](/combat#valor). See [Combat](/combat).

## Demibosses
Demibosses are powerful monsters unique to *Paradise*, built for groups of players. Each one has its own lair, returns hours after it dies, and drops far more loot than an ordinary monster. A demiboss's melee hits do double damage to pets.

| Demiboss | Location |
|:-|:-:|
| [Lich King](/lich_king) | Covetous, floor 3 |

## Dealing with Monsters
- Monsters can be slain with weapons.
- Many animals and monsters can be tamed. See [Animal Taming](/animal_taming) and [Pet](/pet).
- Bards can turn monsters against each other with [Provocation](/provocation), or calm them with [Peacemaking](/peacemaking).

Outside of dungeons, monsters won't initiate an attack against [Young players](/young_player).

## Loot
Monsters carry gold and items, and stronger monsters can drop [treasure maps](/treasure_map). Some loot never drops:
- Necromancer scrolls and reagents
- Seeds
- Fire horns
