# Reactive Armor
Reactive Armor applies a buff that absorbs and reflects melee damage.

**Circle**: 1
**Mana**: 4
**Minimum Absorb**: `1`
**Maximum Absorb**: `min(75, (Magery + Meditation + Inscription) / 3)`
**Reflect**: `20%` of damage absorbed

Reactive Armor lasts until no absorb remains, and cannot be recast if it is in already in effect, even if only 1 absorb remains.
