# Reactive Armor
Reactive Armor absorbs melee damage and reflects part of it back at the attacker. It lasts until its absorb is used up, and can't be recast until then, even with only `1` absorb left.

A caster with `100` Magery and `100` Meditation absorbs up to `50`, and `100` [Inscription](/inscription) raises it to `75`.

| Property | Value |
|:-|:-:|
| Circle | 1 |
| Mana | 4 |
| Minimum Absorb | 1 |
| Maximum Absorb | (Magery + Meditation) / 4<br>+ Inscription / 4 |
| Reflect | 20% of damage absorbed |
