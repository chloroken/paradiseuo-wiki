# Reactive Armor
Reactive Armor gives the caster a buff that absorbs and reflects melee damage. It lasts until no absorb remains, and can't be recast while it's in effect, even if only `1` absorb remains.

| Property | Value |
|:-|:-:|
| Circle | 1 |
| Mana | 4 |
| Minimum Absorb | 1 |
| Maximum Absorb | min(75, (Magery + Meditation + Inscription) / 3) |
| Reflect | 20% of damage absorbed |
