# Item Identification
Item Identification is the skill of recognizing what an item really is. Using the skill and targeting an item within `8` tiles, even one in a container or on a player vendor, has a chance equal to the skill's value of succeeding, so `50` Item Identification succeeds half the time. A success names the item, identifies it, and shows its properties, the same as single-clicking an identified item. A failure gives no information. Targeting a creature or person names it.

Identifying is the only way to learn what a [magic weapon](/magic_weapons), magic armor, wand, or piece of [magic jewelry or clothing](/magic_items) does. Until then, single-clicking it shows only that it's unidentified. A [wand of identification](/magic_items#wands) identifies all of them too.

## Manual Training
Item Identification trains on every attempt, success or failure. Anything works as a target, so a pile of loot and some patience will carry it to grandmaster.
