# Wrestling
Wrestling is the skill of fighting with bare hands. It also decides how well an empty-handed character defends, which matters to mages who keep their hands free for casting.

## Fighting Unarmed
A character with nothing in their hands fights with their fists, using Wrestling to hit. Fists do `1` to `8` damage before bonuses and swing quickly, and Tactics, Strength, and [Anatomy](/anatomy) raise [fist damage](/combat#damage) like any other weapon.

A spellbook or a light can be held without counting as a weapon.

## Wrestling Moves
Fighting with empty hands allows one move, Stun. It needs no button: when a punch hits and the move is off cooldown, it's attempted automatically.

| Move | Used Against | Requirements | Effect |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Stun | Any target, armed or not | 80 Wrestling, 80 Anatomy | Freezes the target in place for 4 seconds |

- Only players can use it.
- It costs no stamina.
- The chance to succeed is `(Wrestling + Anatomy) / 400`: `40%` at `80` in both skills, and `50%` at `100`.
- After any attempt, it can't be used again for `10` seconds.

## Unarmed Defense
A player fighting with bare hands defends with Wrestling alone, so a mage who expects to be caught in melee needs it, and one without it is hit almost every time. Mages who stay out of reach often skip it for a [robe](/meditation#robes) instead.

Monsters defend with whichever is higher: their Wrestling, or `(Anatomy + Evaluating Intelligence + 20) / 2`, up to `120`. A monster mage with `100` [Evaluating Intelligence](/evaluating_intelligence) and little Wrestling still defends as if it had `60` Wrestling.
