# Fencing
Fencing is the skill of fighting with daggers, krysses, and spears. Every fencing weapon can be coated with [poison](/poisoning). The one-handed ones can [ambush](/ambush) from hiding, and the dagger backstabs hardest of all. Two-handed spears pin their targets in place with Paralyzing Blow instead.

## Weapons
| Weapon | Hands | Damage | Effect |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Dagger | One | 4 – 19 | Poison |
| Kryss | One | 3 – 28 | Poison |
| War fork | One | 4 – 32 | Poison |
| Pitchfork | Two | 4 – 16 | Paralyzing Blow, poison |
| Short spear | Two | 4 – 25 | Paralyzing Blow, poison |
| Spear | Two | 2 – 29 | Paralyzing Blow, poison |

The kryss and war fork do the most damage over time of any fencing weapon, a little less than swords with critical strikes, and they're the strongest one-handed weapons without a [weapon effect](/weapon_effects), which suits a fighter who carries a shield and skips [Arms Lore](/arms_lore). The short spear and spear trade some damage for Paralyzing Blow. The pitchfork is weak, and so is the dagger in a long fight, but the dagger lands the hardest ambush of any melee weapon. See [Damage Per Second: Fencing](/dps#fencing).

Tactics, Strength, and Anatomy raise damage the same way as for any other weapon. No scaling skill adds to fencing weapons. See [Combat: Damage](/combat#damage).

## Defense
While holding a fencing weapon, a character defends with Fencing. See [Hit Chance](/combat#hit-chance). The dagger, kryss, and war fork leave the other hand free for a shield. See [Parrying](/parrying).

## Paralyzing Blow
Pitchforks, short spears, and spears land a Paralyzing Blow on `Arms Lore / 400` of hits, `25%` at `100` Arms Lore, freezing the target in place for `2` seconds. Only players land it. See [Weapon Effects: Paralyzing Blow](/weapon_effects#paralyzing-blow).

Hits with these three weapons can train Arms Lore. Hits with daggers, krysses, and war forks can't.

## Poison
Every fencing weapon can be coated with poison, which uses one dose on each hit and can poison the target. Each swing with a poisoned weapon risks poisoning the wielder, less often with more Poisoning. See [Poisoned Weapons](/poisoning#poisoned-weapons).

## Ambush
A fencer with a dagger, kryss, or war fork who attacks from [Hiding](/hiding) does `1.5×` damage, rising to `2.5×` and a hit that can't miss after `10` steps with [Stealth](/stealth). A dagger rises much faster, to `5×`. Two-handed spears are too long to strike with from hiding, so they never ambush. A melee attacker has to reach the target before the stealthed steps run out. See [Ambush](/ambush#stealth).
