# Classes
The era of Ultima Online that *Paradise* targets doesn't have classes (also known as professions) in the traditional sense. Instead, players choose [stats](/stats) and [skills](/skills) to create a custom class, as traditional or unorthodox as they like.

During character creation there is a profession screen, but it should be ignored, and `Advanced` should always be selected.

Although there are no official classes, a warrior, for example, would spend the `225` stat points and `700` skill points on things like Strength, Dexterity, weapon skills, defensive skills, and healing skills.

Builds without Magery get the [Antimage](/combat#antimage) 𐠒 bonuses: better accuracy with Tactics, less physical damage taken, and faster hit point regeneration. The bonuses shrink as Magery rises, so a build with `50` Magery keeps half of each, and a full mage gets none.

The following is a table of ideas. Consider it a starting point.

| Pseudoclass | Skills | Str / Dex / Int | Premise |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Fighter | Parrying, Tactics, Anatomy, Healing, Resisting Spells, Archery, any weapon skill | 100 / 100 / 25 | No magic, so gets the full Antimage bonuses, making it very tanky. Can use any type of weapon with a shield to parry with. Uses ranged weapons to finish fleeing targets, can heal with bandages. Can drop Archery to take a second weapon skill for switching between one-handed and two-handed weapons for a full melee experience. |
| Paladin | Mace Fighting, Tactics, Parrying, Anatomy, Healing, Resisting Spells, 50 Magery, 50 Meditation | 100 / 65 / 60 | Very tanky, uses a mace to drain stamina and a shield to parry with. Can use light magic for buffs and spot-healing, has Meditation to regenerate mana, and can heal with bandages. 50 Magery keeps half of the Antimage bonuses, trading some toughness for utility. |
| Stealth Archer | Archery, Tactics, Anatomy, Healing, Hiding, Stealth, Resisting Spells/Lockpicking | 100 / 100 / 25 | Uses the sneak attack mechanic to do huge burst damage to unsuspecting targets at a range. No Magery, so gets the full Antimage bonuses. Can take Lockpicking over Resisting Spells to trawl dungeons for treasure. |
| Hally Mage | Swordsmanship, Tactics, Wrestling, Magery, Evaluating Intelligence, Meditation, Resisting Spells | 100 / 25 / 100 | Uses spell damage combined with halberd hits to do huge damage. | Pure Mage | Magery, Evaluating Intelligence, Meditation, Resisting Spells, Inscription, Anatomy, flex | 100 / 25 / 100 | A pure caster. Anatomy and Evaluating Intelligence give 110 unarmed defense, which is slightly better than the defense provided by 100 Wrestling. Inscription strengthens Reactive Armor and Protection. The result is a mage with surprisingly high defense for wearing cloth. |
| Tank Mage | Magery, Evaluating Intelligence, Meditation, Resisting Spells, Wrestling, Anatomy, Tactics | 100 / 25 / 100 | Unarmed caster that leverages Wrestling mechanics to stun and damage targets. Wrestling and Anatomy at 80 or more give an automatic stun that stops movement for 4 seconds every 10 seconds at no cost. Tactics makes the punches hit hard, but full Magery means no Antimage accuracy. Can heal and nuke with spells with full competency. Can equip some armor at the cost of Meditation efficacy. |
| Tamer Mage | Animal Taming, Animal Lore, Veterinary, Magery, Meditation, Evaluating Intelligence, Resisting Spells | 100 / 40 / 85 | 40 Dexterity reaches the 4-second bandage breakpoint. | Mapper Bard | Cartography, Lockpicking, Magery, Meditation, Musicianship, Provocation, Peacemaking | 100 / 25 / 100 | Single-handedly covers every step of treasure hunting: farming the maps from monsters, decoding the map, recalling to the map area, picking the lock, disabling the trap, and provoking the guardians onto each other. |
| Pure Bard | Musicianship, Provocation, Peacemaking, Discordance, Archery, Tactics, Healing | 100 / 100 / 25 | A ranged attacker with tons of utility. High adaptability for a non-caster, and gets the full Antimage bonuses. |
| Tamer Bard | Animal Taming, Animal Lore, Veterinary, Musicianship, Provocation, Peacemaking, Hiding | 100 / 100 / 25 | Extremely strong PvMer but lacks travel utility. No Magery, so takes less damage and regenerates faster through Antimage. 100 Dexterity reaches the 3-second bandage breakpoint and adds extra carrying capacity, and Intelligence is useless without Magery anyway. |
| Crafter | Magery + 6 of: Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Carpentry, Cooking, Fletching, Inscription, Tailoring, Tinkering | 100 / 25 / 100 | Takes 100 Intelligence for Magery for travel and Inscription's mana usage. If not taking these skills, use 100 Dexterity instead for the extra carrying capacity. |
| Gatherer | Magery + Fishing, Lumberjacking, Mining, Item Identification, Arms Lore, flex | 100 / 100 / 25 | Uses 100 Dexterity for max carrying capacity, takes Magery for Recall. Can fill in additional crafting skills. |
