# Classes
Ultima Online doesn't have discrete classes. A character's build, or class, is determined by its unique combination of stats, skills, and reputation. 
- [Stats](/stats): Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence, and what each one does.
- [Skills](/skills): every skill, including the ones that are disabled.
- [Reputation](/reputation): builds up passively while interacting with the world.

To play something like an evil warrior, one would spend stat points on Strength and Dexterity and skill points on weapon skills, then commit bad deeds to lower karma.

Every class can drink [potions](/alchemy#potions) to heal and cure, even with full hands. Each class's Healing line lists only what its own skills add.

## Roles
Roles are categories of classes, grouped by purpose or style.
- [Tanks](/tanks) mitigate and heal incoming damage.
  - [Blacksmith](/tanks#blacksmith)
  - [Carpenter](/tanks#carpenter)
  - [Juggernaut](/tanks#juggernaut)
  - [Paladin](/tanks#paladin)
- [Fighters](/fighters) offer melee damage and debuffs.
  - [Armsmaster](/fighters#armsmaster)
  - [Prospecter](/fighters#prospector)
  - [Warrior](/fighters#warrior)
  - [Woodsman](/fighters#woodsman)
- [Archers](/archers) offer safe ranged damage.
  - [Grenadier](/archers#grenadier)
  - [Ranger](/archers#ranger)
- [Stalkers](/stalkers) are masters of subterfuge.
  - [Hunter](/stalkers#hunter)
  - [Puppetmaster](/stalkers#puppetmaster)
  - [Rogue](/stalkers#rogue)
  - [Thief](/stalkers#thief)
- [Mages](/mages) leverage spells for combat and utility.
  - [Battlemage](/mages#battlemage)
  - [Golemancer](/mages#golemancer)
  - [Monk](/mages#monk)
  - [Priest](/mages#priest)
  - [Shepherd](/mages#shepherd)
  - [Summoner](/mages#summoner)
  - [Wizard](/mages#wizard)
- [Tamers](/tamers) capture and control powerful pets.
  - [Beastwarden](/tamers#beastwarden)
  - [Druid](/tamers#druid)
  - [Packleader](/tamers#packleader)
  - [Whisperer](/tamers#whisperer)
- [Bards](/bards) use music to control the battlefield.
  - [Minstrel](/bards#minstrel)
  - [Pirate](/bards#pirate)
  - [Skald](/bards#skald)
- [Crafters](/crafters) harvest resources and produce goods.
  - [Artisan](/crafters#artisan)
  - [Forager](/crafters#forager)
