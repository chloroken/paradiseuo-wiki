# Young Player
New players start with Young status, which protects their characters while they learn the game. Young characters show *(Young)* after their name. Young status is tracked per account, not per character.

## Benefits
- Monsters won't attack a Young player outside of dungeons, unless the Young player attacks them first.
- Young players can't be poisoned.
- When a Young player dies, they keep all of their items in their backpack instead of dropping them on their corpse.
- Healers heal Young players to full health for free when they walk up, once every `5` minutes.
- Young players can log out instantly anywhere instead of needing to wait.
- Young players can travel anywhere by Recall, Gate Travel, and moongates.

## Limits
- Dungeons have no Young protection. Entering one shows a warning, and monsters inside attack as normal. See [Dungeons](/dungeons).
- Young players can't join the Thieves' Guild.
- Pets can't be transferred or friended between a Young player and a player who isn't Young.

## Losing Young Status
Young status ends when any of these happens:
- The account reaches `40` hours of total play time (announced on login), across all of its characters.
- A character raises a skill to `80` or higher while having at least `450` total skill points.
- A player says `I renounce my young player status` and confirms it.

Once lost, Young status can never be regained.
