# Swordsmanship
Swordsmanship is the skill of fighting with bladed weapons: swords, axes, polearms, and knives. It covers more weapon types than any other skill, from swords that land critical strikes to axes that hit hardest of all with [Lumberjacking](/lumberjacking).

## Weapons
| Type | Hands | Weapons | Effect | Scaling Skill |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Swords | One | Broadsword, cutlass, katana,<br>longsword, scimitar, viking sword | Critical strikes | |
| Axes | Two | Axe, battle axe, double axe, executioner's axe,<br>hatchet, large battle axe, two-handed axe | None | Lumberjacking |
| Polearms | Two | Bardiche, halberd | Concussion Blow | |
| Pickaxe | One | Pickaxe | None | [Mining](/mining) |
| Knives | One | Butcher knife, cleaver, skinning knife | Poison | |

- Every sword does about the same damage over time with critical strikes. Slow swords hit in bigger bursts.
- Axes with `100` Lumberjacking do the most damage over time of any weapon, except the hatchet, which is weak.
- Polearms hit hard but slowly, and are the only weapons that land Concussion Blow.
- The pickaxe is weak on its own, but with `100` Mining it does about as much as a sword.
- Knives do less damage, but can be poisoned and can ambush.

Each weapon's damage and speed are listed on [Damage Per Second](/dps). Tactics, Strength, and [Anatomy](/anatomy) raise damage the same way as for any other weapon. See [Combat: Damage](/combat#damage).

## Defense
While holding any of these weapons, a character defends with Swordsmanship. See [Hit Chance](/combat#hit-chance). Swords, knives, and the pickaxe leave the other hand free for a shield. See [Parrying](/parrying).

## Critical Strikes
Swords can land a critical strike, which doubles the hit's damage before armor. Each sword has its own chance, from `10%` for the broadsword to `40%` for the viking sword at `100` [Arms Lore](/arms_lore), scaled down with less Arms Lore. A sword wielder without Arms Lore never lands one. See [Weapon Effects: Critical Strikes](/weapon_effects#critical-strikes).

## Concussion Blow
Polearms land a Concussion Blow on `Anatomy / 400` of hits, `25%` at `100` Anatomy, instead of using Arms Lore. It halves the target's Intelligence and lowers its maximum mana for `30` seconds. See [Weapon Effects: Concussion Blow](/weapon_effects#concussion-blow).

Only players land critical strikes and Concussion Blows. Hits with swords can train Arms Lore. Hits with axes, polearms, knives, and the pickaxe can't.

## Scaling Skills
- Lumberjacking adds up to `30%` damage to axes, except the pickaxe, and hits with them can train Lumberjacking.
- Mining adds up to `100%` damage to the pickaxe.

Only players get these bonuses. See [Weapons: Scaling Skills](/weapons#scaling-skills).

## Knives
Knives are the only Swordsmanship weapons that can be coated with [poison](/poisoning) or used to [ambush](/ambush) from hiding. They never land critical strikes. See [Poisoned Weapons](/poisoning#poisoned-weapons).
