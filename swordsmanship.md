# Swordsmanship
Swordsmanship is the skill of fighting with bladed weapons: swords, axes, polearms, knives, and the pickaxe. It covers more weapon types than any other skill. While holding any of them, a character defends against an attacker's [hit chance](/combat#hit-chance) with Swordsmanship, and swords, knives, and the pickaxe leave the other hand free for [parrying](/parrying) with a shield. Each weapon has its own [speed and damage](/dps), which Tactics, Strength, and [Anatomy](/anatomy) raise the same way as for any other weapon.

| Type | Hands | Weapons | Effect | Scaling Skill |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Swords | One | Broadsword, cutlass, katana,<br>longsword, scimitar, viking sword | [Critical strikes](/weapon_effects#critical-strikes) | |
| Axes | Two | Axe, battle axe, double axe, executioner's axe,<br>hatchet, large battle axe, two-handed axe | | [Lumberjacking](/lumberjacking) |
| Polearms | Two | Bardiche, halberd | [Concussion Blow](/weapon_effects#concussion-blow) | |
| Pickaxe | One | Pickaxe | | [Mining](/mining) |
| Knives | One | Butcher knife, cleaver, skinning knife | [Poison](/poisoning), [ambush](/ambush) | |

- Swords land critical strikes, increasing with skill in [Arms Lore](/arms_lore). Slower swords crit more often, so every sword does about the same damage over time, and the slow ones hit in bigger bursts.
- Axes with `100` Lumberjacking do the most damage over time of any weapon, except the hatchet, which is weak.
- Polearms hit hard but slowly, and land Concussion Blow with Anatomy instead of Arms Lore.
- The pickaxe is weak on its own, but with `100` Mining it does about as much as a sword.
- Knives do less damage, but they're the only Swordsmanship weapons that can be poisoned or ambush from hiding.
