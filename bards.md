# Bards
Bards always take [Musicianship](/musicianship), since every bard skill needs a good performance first.

| Class | Unique Qualities |
|:-|:-:|
| Minstrel | Uses all three bard skills to control a fight |
| Pirate | Runs treasure maps from start to finish on its own |
| Skald | Discords its target, then cuts it down with a two-handed axe |

## Minstrel
The Minstrel is a pure bard and the master of the battlefield. It provokes monsters into fighting each other, calms the ones it doesn't want to deal with, and weakens the rest with [Discordance](/discordance), then finishes them off with a bow or crossbow from afar.

Its music decides which fights happen. [Provocation](/provocation) turns strong monsters into its weapons, [Peacemaking](/peacemaking) stops anything that gets too close, and Discordance lowers a target's stats and skills by up to a fifth. Discordance even works on [demibosses](/demibosses), half the time at grandmaster, so the Minstrel has a role in every boss fight, weakening the boss while its Peacemaking and Provocation handle the boss's [summons](/summon). Its [Tactics](/tactics) gives its arrows [Valor](/valor)'s accuracy.

Without [Anatomy](/anatomy) or [Magery](/magery), its [bandages](/healing) can't cure poison, so it carries cure potions. It has no [Parrying](/parrying) or melee skill, so it relies on its music to keep monsters away.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `100` Dexterity, `25` Intelligence
- Skills: Musicianship, Peacemaking, Provocation, Discordance, Archery, Tactics, and Healing
- Gear: bows, crossbows, and instruments, with studded leather
- Defense: Peacemaking, range, and Valor's accuracy
- Healing: bandages
- Weakness: without Anatomy or Magery it can't cure poison, so it carries cure potions

## Pirate
The Pirate is a treasure hunter who runs treasure maps from start to finish without help. It provokes monsters for map drops, decodes the maps with [Cartography](/cartography), Recalls or Gates to the dig site, and digs up the chest. Then it unlocks the chest with [Lockpicking](/lockpicking) and disarms its trap with [Remove Trap](/remove_trap), provokes the guardians that rise to defend it into fighting each other, and grabs the loot.

Its music and magic do all its fighting. Provocation turns the chest's guardians against one another, and its full Magery gives it travel, utility, and healing spells.

It has almost no fighting ability of its own. With no weapon skill, no [Wrestling](/wrestling), and no Valor, it depends on Provocation working, and a guardian that can't be provoked is a serious problem.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `25` Dexterity, `100` Intelligence
- Skills: Magery, Meditation, Musicianship, Provocation, Cartography, Lockpicking, and Remove Trap
- Gear: instruments and spellbook, with leather
- Defense: Provocation
- Healing: spells
- Weakness: almost no fighting ability of its own

## Skald
The Skald is a war bard: a Viking-style warrior who sings its enemies weak, then cuts them down. It discords its target first, lowering its stats and skills, and then fights it with a two-handed axe and full Valor, so the weakened monster hits softer and is easier to hit.

Bard skills only need an instrument in the backpack, so it plays without putting its axe away. Axes have no weapon effect, so it needs no [Arms Lore](/arms_lore). Its Discordance works on demibosses too, half the time at grandmaster, which makes it a valuable fighter in boss fights. Its Anatomy gives it regeneration and bandages that cure poison.

It has no [Resisting Spells](/resisting_spells) and no Magery, so casters hurt and it walks everywhere. A two-handed axe means no shield, and half its attempts to discord a demiboss fail, so it doesn't always get its edge.

- Stats: `100` Strength, `100` Dexterity, `25` Intelligence
- Skills: Musicianship, Discordance, Swordsmanship, Tactics, Anatomy, Parrying, and Healing
- Gear: two-handed axe and instrument, with plate
- Defense: Swordsmanship, full Valor, and a weakened target
- Healing: bandages
- Weakness: no Resisting Spells or Magery, so casters hurt, and half its attempts to discord a demiboss fail
