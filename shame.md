# Shame
Shame is a [dungeon](/dungeons) south of [Yew](/towns) with four floors. It is the a hotbed of elemental activity: earth, air, water, fire, deadly poison elementals, the exclusive blood elemental, and their ostensible leader: the [Greater Elemental](/greater_elemental).

Elementals in Shame have a chance to drop a summon scroll on top of their normal loot, unless they were summoned themselves. See [Summon: Summon Scrolls](/summon#summon-scrolls).

## History
Shame lies in the western mountains of Britannia, and it is bound to the virtue of [Honor](https://wiki.ultimacodex.com/wiki/Honor). It began as a mine, abandoned when gremlins overran it, and the Avatar later braved its depths for the [Purple Stone of Honor](https://wiki.ultimacodex.com/wiki/Virtue_Stones). In time, miners returned to dig again, and some never came back up. Mages followed them down in search of power, binding elementals to guard their towers. After years of digging and foul experimentation, something started killing the mages stationed in the deepest sections of the dungeon. Anything capable of slaughtering such capable adversaries must be formidable indeed.

## Floor 1
Floor 1 is filled with scorpions, earth elementals, and the skeletons of miners still haunting their forgotten campsites.

## Floor 2
Elementals have overrun floor 2. Air elementals roam freely, water elementals patrol the river, and earth elementals wander the corridors. Skeletons are all that remain of those who tried to stay.

## Floor 3
A variety of creatures fill the nooks and crannies of floor 3: air, fire, water, and earth elementals, giant spiders, scorpions, sea serpents, and skeletons.

### Towers
Two towers flank the entrance to floor 3. Deranged evil mages, evil mage lords, and slimes guard them. A magical teleporter joins the towers, guarded by poison elementals.

## Fortress
A fortress is embedded in the mountain, separating floors 3 and 4. Teleporters marked by silver moongates lead in and out, and gazers guard the entrance. In the fortress cells are elder gazers and skeletons.

## Floor 4
On floor 4, the mountains carve into a lagoon where krakens and sea serpents swim. Air, earth, fire, water, and poison elementals cover the entire floor. Two gazer nests survive, consisting of gazers, gazer larvae, and elder gazers. Giant spiders and scorpions take refuge in the corners of the caves.

A single tower stands on floor 4, home to skeletons and bone magi. The bone magi were likely evil mages before something killed them. In the back of the floor is a cave branch full of blood elementals, the result of wicked experimentation.

A series of ropes, starting at a pair of red braziers on the shore, carries adventurers to most of the islands in the lagoon. At the center of the lagoon drifts the Greater Elemental. Could it be the source of the mages' failures?

## Treasure
Dungeon chests spawn on every floor of Shame except the fortress. See [Lockpicking: Dungeon Chests](/lockpicking#dungeon-chests).
