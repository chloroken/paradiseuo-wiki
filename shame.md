# Shame
Shame is a [dungeon](/dungeons) south of [Yew](/towns) with four floors. It is a hotbed of elemental activity: earth, air, water, fire, deadly poison elementals, the exclusive blood elementals, and their ostensible leader: the [Greater Elemental](/greater_elemental).

Elementals in Shame have a chance to drop a summon scroll on top of their normal loot, unless they were summoned themselves. See [Summon: Summon Scrolls](/summon#summon-scrolls).

## History
Shame lies in the western mountains of Britannia, and it is bound to the virtue of [Honor](https://wiki.ultimacodex.com/wiki/Honor). It began as a mine, but was abandoned when overrun by gremlins. In time, miners returned to dig again, but most never came back up. Some say those miners still patrol their old camps.

But it wasn't just miners interested in Shame. A band of evil mages, outcast from society, traveled down in search of power, binding elementals to guard their towers to sow their foul experimentations in safety. But the elementals had their own ideas.

## Floor 1
Floor 1 is filled with scorpions, earth elementals, and the skeletons of miners still haunting their forgotten campsites.

## Floor 2
Elementals have overrun floor 2. Air elementals roam freely, water elementals patrol the river, and earth elementals wander the corridors. Skeletons are all that remain of those who tried to stay. Spike traps and saw traps line its paths.

## Floor 3
A variety of creatures fill the nooks and crannies of floor 3: air, fire, water, and earth elementals, giant spiders, scorpions, sea serpents, and skeletons. It is the most heavily trapped floor in Shame, with saw traps, spike traps, and gas traps. A secret door hides in its walls.

### Towers
Two towers flank the entrance to floor 3. Deranged evil mages, evil mage lords, and slimes guard them. A magical teleporter joins the towers, guarded by poison elementals.

### Fortress
A fortress is embedded in the mountain, separating floors 3 and 4. Teleporters marked by silver moongates lead in and out, and gazers guard the entrance. In the fortress cells are elder gazers and skeletons.

## Floor 4
On floor 4, the mountains carve into a lagoon where krakens and sea serpents swim. Air, earth, fire, water, and poison elementals cover the entire floor. Two gazer nests survive, consisting of gazers, gazer larvae, and elder gazers. Giant spiders and scorpions take refuge in the corners of the caves, along with mushroom traps.

A single tower stands on floor 4, home to skeletons and bone magi. The bone magi were likely evil mages before something killed them. In the back of the floor is a cave branch where the mages did their experimenting, now full of blood elementals.

A series of ropes, starting at a pair of red braziers on the shore, carries adventurers to most of the islands in the lagoon. At the center of the water drifts the Greater Elemental, surrounded by water elementals and krakens. Could it be the source of the mages' failures?

## Treasure
Dungeon chests spawn on every floor of Shame except the fortress. See [Lockpicking: Dungeon Chests](/lockpicking#dungeon-chests).
