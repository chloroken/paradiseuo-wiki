# Blacksmithing
Blacksmithing is the skill of forging metal weapons, armor, and shields from [ingots](/mining#smelting), using a smith's hammer or tongs within `2` tiles of an anvil and a forge. Like every [crafting](/crafting) skill, each item has its own skill range and can come out exceptional. Blacksmiths also repair metal gear and melt it back down into ingots. Outside the forge, Blacksmithing lets two-handed maces [splash their Crushing Blows](/weapons#scaling-skills) onto nearby monsters.

## Metal Types
Ingots come in nine metals, chosen in the crafting menu. Iron needs no extra skill, but each colored metal needs a minimum Blacksmithing to work at all. Colored metal doesn't change an item's success chance or its materials.

| Metal | Blacksmithing Needed |
|:-|:-:|
| Iron | 0 |
| Dull copper | 65 |
| Shadow iron | 70 |
| Copper | 75 |
| Bronze | 80 |
| Gold | 85 |
| Agapite | 90 |
| Verite | 95 |
| Valorite | 99 |

Armor and shields forged from colored metal [ward off magic](/armor#metal-types), reducing spell damage more for rarer metals. Weapons forged from colored metal only take its color.

## Repairing
Repairing from the crafting menu restores any weapon or armor that Blacksmithing can make, as long as it's in the blacksmith's backpack and an anvil and forge are near. Leather, studded, and bone armor, and weapons made by other crafters, can't be repaired. A poisoned weapon must be wiped clean first.

The more worn the item, the harder the repair. A grandmaster always succeeds on an item that has lost up to `80%` of its durability, and succeeds half the time on one that's fully broken. A failed repair restores nothing.

Every attempt, successful or not, has a chance to lower the item's maximum durability, starting at `40%`. The chance rises by `1%` for each point of durability the item has lost, and drops by `1%` for every `10` Blacksmithing. Each time it happens, maximum durability drops by `1` at `90` Blacksmithing or higher, `2` from `70`, and `3` below that. An item with too little maximum durability left can't be repaired again. Armor also loses maximum durability each time it [breaks](/armor#durability).

## Smelting Items
Smelting from the crafting menu melts a metal weapon or piece of armor back into ingots of its metal, at a forge. A crafted item returns `66%` of the ingots it took, rounded down. Bought and looted items return only `1` ingot. Colored items need the same Blacksmithing or [Mining](/mining) as their metal.

## Manual Training
Blacksmithing trains by crafting whatever the smith succeeds at about half the time, moving up the tables as the skill rises. Smelting the results returns two thirds of the iron, so a stack of ingots goes a long way.
