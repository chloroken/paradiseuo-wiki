# Ultima Online
Ultima Online is a sandbox: there are no levels, no set path, and no wrong way to play within the shard's rules. Players go on adventures, hoard treasure, and construct their own houses.

## Choosing Skills
A character may have up to `700` skill points spread across its skills. Skills work best in combinations that support each other, and a character who spreads too thin ends up good at nothing. A mage wants Evaluating Intelligence for spell damage and Meditation for mana, a tamer wants Animal Lore to control pets and Veterinary to heal them, and a blacksmith needs a way to get ore, usually Mining. The [class](/classes) ideas are starting points, and [Skills](/skills) lists every skill.

## Training Skills
Many townspeople teach the basics of their trade for a fee. Saying `<name> train` near one lists the skills they teach, and dropping the gold they ask for on them pays for a lesson. Gold in the bank is taken out by saying `withdraw <amount>` near a banker, one of many speech [commands](/commands).

Past the basics, a skill rises by using it. Each skill page explains how it works and where its gains come from.

## First Adventures
New characters start as [Young players](/young_player). Outside of dungeons, monsters leave a Young player alone unless the player attacks first, and a Young player who dies keeps everything in their backpack. Young status ends after `40` hours of play, or once a character raises a skill to `80` with `450` total skill points, so it's the time to learn the game.

The wilds around towns hold weak monsters such as rats and slimes, a safe place to try out new skills. [Dungeons](/dungeons) have no Young protection at all, and their monsters attack anyone, so they're worth waiting for until a character can handle itself. [Monsters](/monsters) covers what's out there.

Escorting NPCs between towns and freeing prisoners from camps give a new character reasons to travel the land through [quests](/world#quests).

## Getting Around
Many [towns](/towns) have a moongate, which links them together. A horse [mount](/mount) can be bought from any animal trainer, and Magery's Recall spell is the fastest way to travel, though it doesn't work in dungeons or the Lost Lands.

Inside a [house](/house) they own or are friends of, characters log out instantly. Elsewhere, logging out takes a short while, unless the character is still Young.

## Equipment
Better weapons and armor come from loot, from crafters, and from NPC vendors. Looted weapons can be [magic](/magic_weapons), and [crafted](/crafting) ones can be exceptional.

Once Young status ends, a character who dies drops everything on their corpse, starting items included. Anything too valuable to risk is safest in the bank.

## Death
A dead character becomes a ghost and has to be resurrected, by a healer NPC or by another player with [Healing](/healing). Every resurrection costs `10%` of the character's [Fame](/reputation).

## Staying Safe
Outside duel arenas and guild wars, players can't attack each other on *Paradise*, but scams still exist. A deal that sounds too good to be true usually is, and a gate cast by a stranger can lead somewhere dangerous. Other players are often happy to help, and staff can be reached through the [community](/community) channels.
