# Guilds
*Paradise* has two kinds of guilds. Player guilds are founded and run by players, while the NPC guilds are trade unions run by guildmasters in town. A character can belong to one of each.

## Player Guilds
Player guilds bring friends together under one name. Guild wars are one of the only ways players can fight each other, as covered on [Rules](/rules). Saying `I resign from my guild` leaves a player guild.

## NPC Guilds
Each NPC guild asks its members for skill in its own trade.

| Guild | Skill |
|:-|:-:|
| Bards' Guild | Musicianship |
| Blacksmiths' Guild | Blacksmithing |
| Fishermen's Guild | Fishing |
| Healers' Guild | Healing |
| Mages' Guild | Magery |
| Merchants' Guild | Item Identification |
| Miners' Guild | Mining |
| Rangers' Guild | Archery |
| Tailors' Guild | Tailoring |
| Thieves' Guild | Stealing |
| Tinkers' Guild | Tinkering |
| Warriors' Guild | Tactics |

Only the [Thieves' Guild](#thieves-guild) gives its members anything for now. The other guilds have no benefits yet.

### Joining
Saying the guildmaster's name and `join`, such as `Kaspar join`, from within `2` tiles asks to join. A character needs at least `50` in the guild's skill, counting only base skill, and can only belong to one NPC guild at a time. A guildmaster who accepts names a fee of `500` gold, and dropping exactly that much on them completes the joining. A refused fee is handed back.

A character below the skill floor is told *Thou must be at least adept at Stealing to join my guild.*, naming the guild's skill.

### Resigning
Saying the guildmaster's name and `resign` leaves the guild. A new member can't resign until `7` days after joining, and only after `4` days of play time as a member.

## Thieves' Guild
The Thieves' Guild guildmaster keeps to the Pirate's Den in [Buccaneer's Den](/towns). Besides `50` Stealing, they turn away [Young](/young_player) players and anyone with murder counts.

Members can:
- [Steal](/stealing) from townsfolk, and fill their pockets
- [Peek](/snooping#peeking-into-backpacks) into townsfolk's backpacks, even in guarded towns
- Buy a disguise kit for `700` gold, by saying `disguise` near the guildmaster and then dropping the gold on them
