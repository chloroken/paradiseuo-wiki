# Lockpicking
Lockpickers use [lockpicks](/Lockpick) to.. pick locks. These locks often secure containers full of loot which can be taken by the lockpicker. A [Skeleton Key](/Skeleton_Key) can be used instead of a lockpick for better success rates.

## Town Chests
There are `Town Chests` scattered throughout buildings in towns. The difficult of these chests is tied to the NPCs that live in the building. Generally, the loot inside will be related to the type of building the chest is in, a chest in an Alchemist might contain potions, for example.

| Chest Level | Min Skill | Max Skill | NPC types |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-|
| **Level 1** | 0 | 30 | Alchemist, Baker, Bard, Butcher, Carpenter, Clothier, Cobbler, Docks, Farm, Healer, Herbalist, Inn, Library, Merchant, Mill, Mine, Observatory, Painter, Stables, Tavern, Thief Guild, Tinker, Veterinarian |
| **Level 2** | 30 | 60 | Armorer, Blacksmith, Bowyer, Jeweler, Mage, Ranger, Tanner, Weaponsmith, Provisioner |
| **Level 3** | 60 | 90 | Fighters Guild, Guards |

## Dungeon Chests
`Dungeon Chests` are found within dungeons and have traditional dungeon loot. They range from level 1 (graveyards, orc camps) to level 2-3 (dungeons) to level 4 (deep, difficult dungeons).

| Chest Level | Min Skl | Max Skill |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| **Level 1** | 43 | 52 |
| **Level 2** | 68 | 72 |
| **Level 3** | 76 | 84 |
| **Level 4** | 82 | 92 |

## Treasure Chests
[Treasure Maps](/Treasure_Map) spawn a level 1-5 `Treasure Chest`.

| Chest Level | Min Lockpicking | Max Lockpicking | Gain Limit |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| **Level 1** | 0 | 10 | 50 |
| **Level 2** | 40 | 50 | 90 |
| **Level 3** | 60 | 70 | 100 |
| **Level 4** | 75 | 85 | 100 |
| **Level 5** | 90 | 100 | 100 |
